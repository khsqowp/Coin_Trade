# KR/US stock and ETF data path check

Checked: 2026-11-19. Scope: identify a lawful daily-history route capable of supporting the frozen KR/US portfolio study. This is a provider/readiness review, not a strategy backtest, account creation, purchase, quote request, or API-key request.

## Decision

**No KR/US strategy backtest is authorized by the evidence currently available.** A plausible domestic daily-data path now exists through KRX OPEN API, but fields, adjustment treatment, delisted coverage, sample contents, price, and the compatibility of the user's requested duplicate storage with the API's no-third-party-provision rule remain unverified. CRSP is a strong US research-data candidate, but current subscription pricing and a usable sample were not publicly confirmed.

## Korea — KRX OPEN API: conditional candidate

- KRX's official API catalog lists daily trading information for KOSPI, KOSDAQ, KONEX, and ETF. The catalog says the KOSPI and KOSDAQ daily series start on 2010-01-04, KONEX on 2013-07-01, and ETF on 2010-01-04. [Official KRX service catalog](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/INFO/service/OPPINFO004.cmd)
- Access requires a KRX Data Marketplace account, authentication-key request, and API service application/approval. The published terms say a key is valid for one year, can be extended, and is limited to 10,000 requests per key per day. The terms restrict use to non-commercial purposes and prohibit providing received information to third parties; they also say the information may no longer be used after the API agreement ends. [Official KRX API use instructions](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/INFO/OPPINFO003.jsp), [official terms](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/INFO/OPPINFO002.jsp)
- The API details page describes daily stock trading data from 2010, but its downloadable field specification and sample response were not exposed in this read-only public-page check. Adjusted OHLC, dividend/split treatment, inactive/delisted history, stable security identifiers, and the exact ETF universe therefore remain **unverified**. [Official KOSPI daily API page](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/USES/service/OPPUSES002_S2.cmd?BO_ID=JvJFzlAENzZlPBDNGAWC)
- KRX's separate Data Marketplace purchase route says product detail pages provide period, fields, sample files, and prices after member login; KRX states academic purchases by eligible students, faculty, and institutions receive a 50% discount. No product-specific daily-stock/ETF price was publicly visible in this check. [Official purchase guide](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/DATA/OPPDATA001.jsp)

**Use decision:** keep KRX as the first low-cost domestic sample to investigate, but do not treat it as a validated backtest panel until a permitted sample establishes field semantics, inactive-security coverage, corporate actions, and reproducible downloads. The API's non-commercial and non-sharing restrictions appear compatible with a private personal research workflow, but the planned separate Mac/Oracle copies are not cleared by this research and must not be assumed permitted.

## United States — CRSP US Stock Databases: best-fit paid candidate

- Morningstar/CRSP states that its US Stock Databases provide daily/monthly market data and corporate actions for more than 36,000 active and inactive securities, with permanent PERMNO/PERMCO identifiers. This directly addresses the survivor-bias and identity-history requirements better than the current unverified public panels. [Official CRSP product page](https://indexes.morningstar.com/research-data-products/crsp-us-stock-databases)
- The CRSP CIZ guide documents daily total-return, price-return, delisting-return, daily OHLC, volume, and security-subtype fields including ETF/ETN. ETF-specific historical coverage, licensing for personal self-directed trading research, complete sample contents, and current price still require confirmation from a licensed sample or subscription response. [Official CRSP CIZ guide](https://www.crsp.org/wp-content/uploads/guides/CRSP_US_Stock_%26_Indexes_Database_Guide_Flat_File_Format_2.0.pdf)
- The current official product page directs prospective customers to request subscription information; no public list price was found. A WRDS product page exposes a CRSP sample listing, but the reviewed page does not establish that the sample is an unrestricted, downloadable evaluation dataset or that it includes the required daily stock/ETF panel. [Official subscription information](https://indexes.morningstar.com/research-data-products/crsp-us-stock-databases), [WRDS CRSP listing](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/data-vendors/center-for-research-in-security-prices-crsp/)

**Use decision:** CRSP is a strong schema/coverage fit if a personal or eligible institutional license and price are acceptable. Do not buy, create a WRDS account, or treat the listed CRSP sample as adequate until rights, actual daily sample coverage, and price are confirmed.

## Remaining gates before strategy work

1. For KRX, obtain a read-only sample/specification without creating an account or key; if unavailable, the user must decide whether to authorize the required account/key and service application. Test whether OHLC adjustment and delisted/inactive history exist before calculating any strategy outcomes.
2. For US, obtain an official CRSP daily stock/ETF sample and written subscription quote under the allowed sample/quote scope; no data purchase or account creation is authorized here.
3. For both markets, verify exact fields, rights, PIT security membership, delisted outcomes, corporate actions, version/hash lineage, execution costs/taxes, and whether local/server duplication is permitted.
4. Only after all data gates pass, preregister a nonduplicate daily-candle strategy and apply the frozen OOS gates: net CAGR ≥20%, MDD ≤30%, Sharpe ≥1, and ≥200 closed OOS round trips. Do not substitute a current-survivor sample or an unadjusted close series.

## Addendum — public-page checks (2026-11-19)

- **KRX delisting evidence improved, but the API gate remains open.** KRX's public ETF security finder has an “include delisted securities” option, and its delisted-security price-trend page exposes date, code, market, close, open/high/low, volume, value, and market capitalization fields. This proves the public site has a delisted lookup/report path; it does **not** prove complete ETF lifetime coverage, downloadable history, adjustment/dividend treatment, reproducibility, or that the separately approved daily API includes those inactive securities. [ETF finder](https://data.krx.co.kr/comm/finder/finder_dataetfisu.jsp), [delisted-security price trend](https://data.krx.co.kr/contents/MDC/STAT/issue/MDCSTAT239.jsp)
- **KRX API gates remain material.** The official instructions require account/login, key approval, and per-service application approval; the public sample-call interface still requests an API key, so anonymous API sample retrieval is not demonstrated. [KRX use instructions](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/INFO/OPPINFO003.jsp), [KRX daily-series service page](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/USES/service/OPPUSES001_S2.cmd?BO_ID=SsgXTEspyJESKvyXZtCU)
- **CRSP ETF schema evidence improved.** The CRSP official share-code table explicitly identifies share code 73 as ETF, and the CIZ cross-reference includes `SecurityType`/`SecuritySubType` fields. This establishes that ETF classification exists in CRSP's schema, not the completeness of ETF coverage or its treatment in a proposed subscription. The official product page remains daily/monthly and subscription-based. [CRSP share-code table](https://www.crsp.org/wp-content/uploads/ShareCode.html), [CIZ cross-reference guide](https://www.crsp.org/wp-content/uploads/guides/CRSP_Cross_Reference_Guide_1.0_to_2.0.pdf), [CRSP product page](https://indexes.morningstar.com/research-data-products/crsp-us-stock-databases)
- **The WRDS sample catalog is not yet an adequate stock/ETF evaluation sample.** It lists “CRSP Sample” as 351.4 MiB with stock-price concepts, but gives no identifiers and does not specify security coverage or make clear that the listing itself is a freely downloadable file. WRDS separately says full-function queries and complete data are subscriber-only. Therefore sample suitability and access remain unverified. [WRDS CRSP catalog](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/data-vendors/center-for-research-in-security-prices-crsp/), [WRDS sample-data page](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/demo-wrds/)

**Updated decision:** these public-page checks strengthen KRX/CRSP as candidate sources; they do not clear license, sample, point-in-time universe, price, corporate-action, or local/server-copy gates. No accounts, keys, requests, purchases, downloads, or backtests were performed. No equity performance backtest is yet validly supported.

### WRDS public demo sample inspection

The CRSP demo does expose an actual sample output without a subscription login: a fixed monthly CRSP stock extract for four named equities (JJSF, IBM, MSFT, DELL), January–December 2006, with 48 observations and 12 fields including PERMNO, ticker, price, delisting code, distribution code, and market return. It is useful for checking output format and some identifier/event fields, but it is **monthly**, contains no ETF in the demonstrated cohort, and is not enough to validate daily OHLC/ETF corporate-action coverage or strategy performance. WRDS states that full-function queries and complete data are subscriber-only. [WRDS demo description](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/demo-wrds/), [sample request summary](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/demo/crsp/result/), [sample output](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/demo/crsp/data/)

This narrows the US acquisition route: public sample visibility is confirmed, but a daily stock/ETF sample and personal-use license/price are still unverified. No sample file was downloaded or used in a backtest.

## US alternative — Norgate Data Platinum (public terms/pricing fit; acquisition not authorized)

- Norgate's US Stock Market package covers listed security types including ETFs/ETNs. Its Platinum tier adds delisted securities and historical index constituents and reaches back to 1990; Diamond reaches 1950. Public pricing is USD 346.50 for six months or USD 630 for twelve months for Platinum. These are current provider-listed prices, not a quote for a particular tax/jurisdiction case. [US package/prices](https://norgatedata.com/stockmarketpackages.php)
- Norgate's content table explicitly lists ETFs among US security types and lists ETFs/ETNs among the delisted types. It also disclaims complete delisted coverage, particularly in early historical periods. Its FAQ says daily OHLCV can be configured as raw, split-adjusted, split+special-distribution-adjusted, or total-return-adjusted (ordinary dividends included). [US content table](https://norgatedata.com/data-content-tables.php), [data/adjustment FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/data-package-faq.php)
- Norgate's terms are unusually well matched to the requested private Mac+server workflow: while a personal-use subscription is active, the license permits export/extraction, copying, storage, backup, and processing on separate computers, Mac/Linux, private cloud, or remote server, as long as the data stays solely for the individual's own personal research/trading and is not made available to others. On expiry, exported/extracted data must be deleted unless renewal is imminent; retained backups are not a perpetual license. [Official subscription FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/faq.php), [EULA](https://norgatedata.com/subscribe/eula.php)
- Point-in-time scope is not universal: Norgate offers daily historical constituents for selected indices, but the project still has to choose a universe and use dated constituent time series rather than a current/past watchlist. Not every index includes ETFs. A general “all stocks and ETFs investable at each date” universe is not established just by buying Platinum. [Norgate constituent documentation](https://norgatedata.com/data-content-tables.php), [NDU FAQ](https://norgatedata.com/ndu-faq.php)
- Operational constraint: Norgate Data Updater is Windows software (a Windows PC or VM is needed for source access/update/export). Norgate supports exporting historical price data to ASCII/CSV, but only price data—not all other data features—is exportable that way. The point-in-time index-membership features may require a supported NDU integration; a Mac/Linux price-only extract alone should not be assumed to include that universe history. Norgate also says historical records can be revised while a subscription is active and it provides no data-versioning mechanism, so a research snapshot would need an internally generated content manifest/hash while retention remains licensed. [NDU overview](https://norgatedata.com/ndu-overview.php), [export details](https://norgatedata.com/)

**Updated US decision:** Norgate Platinum is now the best publicly evidenced candidate for the user's *personal* daily US stocks/ETF backtests and local+private-server processing, subject to validating a permitted sample, exact historical ETF/delist coverage, point-in-time universe fields/export path, and data lineage. It is not intraday data and is not evidence of strategy performance. No account, trial, purchase, or data download was initiated. Sharadar remains a weaker fit: despite daily stock/fund coverage, its current personal license restricts use to personal non-professional activity, bars sharing/access by others, requires deletion of all data copies within 30 days after termination, and prohibits disclosure of evaluation conclusions to outside parties without prior written approval. [Sharadar data description](https://sharadar.com/prices), [current terms](https://sharadar.com/terms)

## KRX local/server rights clarification

KRX's API terms restrict use to non-commercial purposes and prohibit providing received information to third parties. The API's public language reviewed here does not explicitly resolve whether one individual's privately controlled Mac and dedicated server may both hold/process the data, whether the server operator's access counts as third-party provision, or how retained backups must be handled after the agreement ends. The separate Data Marketplace site terms also prohibit unauthorized copying/reproduction and automated collection. Therefore no public-screen scraping or duplicate Mac/server storage is assumed permitted; this requires written KRX clarification before acquisition. [KRX API terms](https://openapi.krx.co.kr/contents/OPP/INFO/OPPINFO002.jsp), [Data Marketplace terms](https://data.krx.co.kr/contents/MDC/INFO/informationController/MDCINFO003.cmd)
