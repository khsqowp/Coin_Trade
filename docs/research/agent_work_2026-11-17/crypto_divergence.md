# Crypto divergence: additional tests and implementation risks

Scope: read-only review of `SPEC.md`, the 2026-11-17 divergence preregistration, `setups.py`, P5–P8 summaries, and R018 gate artifacts. No market labels/outcomes were opened or scored. Existing source was not modified. No order, API, realtime, or manual-signal activity is in scope.

R018 is not fully cleared for market-label access: the checkout contains the Profile-A/null synthetic result, but no Profile-B/C, alternative, missingness-stress, or separate OHLC-path method-gate result artifacts were present in the run directory. The recorded smoke is explicitly incomplete. Keep every market-result label and score closed until the entire synthetic method gate is completed and independently reviewed.

## Additional unit/property tests

1. **Pivot anchor versus confirmation index**
   - For lag `L ∈ {2,3,4}`, construct unique low/high pivots with known RSI(14) and MACD-histogram values at anchor `a`; assert the emitted divergence compares oscillator values at `a`, not at confirmation `a+L`.
   - Change only oscillator values on confirmation/future bars while holding anchor values fixed; signal identity and direction must not change.
   - Check repeated/equal extrema, simultaneous high/low pivots, NaNs/warm-up, and endpoints. Assert each pivot is emitted once at the registered confirmation time and never at its anchor.
   - Add a regression fixture proving current `rsi_divergence` behavior (sample `r[i]` at confirmation index) differs from the preregistered anchor-aligned rule; prevent accidental reuse of the old implementation as the new candidate.

2. **Confirmation, availability, and prefix invariance**
   - For every prefix ending before pivot confirmation, assert that the pivot and any dependent divergence are absent. At confirmation, assert they become available exactly once.
   - For every anchor time `t`, compare outputs from the original series prefix through `t` with the same prefix after arbitrary mutation/appending of all later OHLCV rows; all emitted signals, stops, ATR, RSI/MACD and closed-4h features through `t` must be identical.
   - Inject explicit future-peeking mutations (future extreme that would invalidate an earlier pivot; future oscillator value; future 4h close/EMA) and assert the causal guard fails each deliberately corrupted implementation.
   - Verify tie semantics in pivot windows (`>=`/`<=`) deterministically and document which equal-high/equal-low bar owns the pivot; no duplicate-anchor or order-dependent result.

3. **Next executable entry and entry variants**
   - For a closed signal candle at index `i`, assert market entry uses the next executable bar open, never `close[i]` or the pivot price. Include session/gap/open discontinuity and final-bar cases; a signal without a subsequent executable bar is not emitted/scored.
   - For stop-entry, test trigger strictly after the signal bar, direction-specific break (`high` long / `low` short), three-bar validity, expiry boundary, gap-through trigger, and cases where entry opens already beyond stop/target. Freeze whether fill is at trigger or gap open; conservative executable-price handling must not grant favorable fills.
   - Assert baseline and candidate comparisons use identical eligible anchors and entry timestamps; no baseline may copy candidate direction mechanically.

4. **4h macro alignment to lower-timeframe bars**
   - Build synthetic timestamps straddling a 4h boundary. A lower-timeframe signal may use only the latest fully closed 4h candle; the currently forming 4h candle must be unavailable.
   - Test exact-boundary timestamps, UTC/day rollover, missing 4h candle, duplicate timestamp, and lower-bar aggregation. Assert no forward-fill across a missing/invalid 4h interval and no lookahead from the next completed 4h bar.
   - Verify EMA(50)/EMA(200) warm-up, close-vs-EMA and EMA ordering, mirrored long/short predicates, and that all constituent lower-timeframe bars used in the 4h candle are closed before macro state is observable.

5. **Barrier labels, 10/20-bar horizons, and direction metric separation**
   - Table-test long/short symmetry for target-first, stop-first, neither-hit timeout, invalidated-before-entry, target/stop both touched within the same minimum path segment, and entry already through a barrier. Same-segment ties and ambiguous ordering must resolve conservatively or become unresolved per frozen contract; never infer favorable intraminute order from OHLC alone.
   - Assert 10-native-bar and 20-native-bar outcomes are separately keyed claims with the correct inclusive/exclusive deadline convention. A 10-bar timeout cannot borrow later 20-bar success; 20-bar outcomes cannot be clipped to 10 bars.
   - Verify M2 denominator is all emitted forecasts with resolved labels, including overlaps. Separately test the outcome-blind per-symbol non-overlap selector: earliest event is retained, later events are skipped through its registered deadline, ties are stable, and only this subset supplies N≥200 and ≥20-symbol eligibility.
   - Compute deadline-close directional accuracy independently from target-first M2. Fixtures where direction is correct but stop hits first, and where target hits first but deadline close reverses, must yield distinct metrics.

6. **Costs, R accounting, and gap fail-closed behavior**
   - Test round-trip 0.10% fee exactly once per completed trade (entry + exit accounting must not double-charge); report the registered adverse 2x-slippage stress separately. Costs must be applied to actual executable entry/exit prices and consistently to candidate and matched signal baselines.
   - Property-test net-R invariance to uniform price scaling and correct sign/magnitude for long/short, each stop buffer, and each target multiple. Reject nonpositive risk distance, nonfinite values, impossible stop side, and invalid parameter combinations rather than clipping them silently.
   - Generate missing, duplicate, non-monotonic, and discontinuous 1m candles inside every prospective path, including across timeframe boundaries. Any path whose required minute coverage is incomplete is `UNRESOLVED_DATA`; do not bridge, interpolate, drop as a non-signal, or count as a win/loss in the primary resolved-label M2. Enforce the preregistered unresolved-rate/coverage gate before a claim can be eligible; also retain the specified all-unresolved-as-loss sensitivity.
   - Verify manifest/data fingerprints include exact source files, symbol/time coverage, aggregation code, and accepted/rejected archive mask. Changed inputs or code must invalidate cached signals/labels and create a distinct trial identity.

7. **Walk-forward, permutation, and neighbor-gate properties**
   - Use calendar timestamps (not bar counts) to assert five contiguous 504-day train / next 252-day test windows, 252-day advances, identical boundaries across timeframes, maximum-20-native-bar purge, indicator warm-up, and preservation/reporting of empty windows. Insufficient 1,764-day usable history plus warm-up must return `INCONCLUSIVE`, never shorten windows.
   - Test that each test window is scored only with training-selected frozen parameters; mutate test-window labels and assert training selection, neighbor pass decisions, and selected parameters do not change.
   - For 1,000 deterministic trade-order permutations, assert reproducibility from recorded seed, conservation of the complete realized net-R multiset, fixed 1% risk convention, correct equity-curve MDD, and reported historical / median / P95 / maximum MDD. Do not interpret this as a market-path or portfolio-dependence model.
   - Enumerate one-step neighbors from the frozen grid mechanically. Assert under-sampled/unresolved neighbors fail; fewer than four neighbors is non-evaluable; robustness requires ≥80% pass and adjacent median M2 within 10 percentage points. Test grid edges, duplicate cells, and no post-test retuning.

## Backtest implementation risks

- **Existing rule is not the preregistered rule.** `rsi_divergence` in `chart_predictor/setups.py` consumes pivots at their confirmation indices but compares `r[i]` at that same index. The preregistration samples oscillator at each pivot anchor, uses explicit lag 2/3/4, adds closed-4h regime alignment and new execution/label variants. Reusing P5 signal cache/results would therefore conflate materially different rules.
- **Prior evidence is not clean confirmation.** P5–P8 are prior development-window setup studies under their then-current 75%/30-event screening and common 2R/3R trade framework; the divergence protocol freezes M2 ≥80%, separate non-overlap N≥200 and ≥20 symbols, 10/20 horizons, cost stress, 504/252 WFA, 1,000 permutations and neighbor robustness. Preserve old trials separately; do not merge their scores, labels, or cells into this protocol. Existing P5/P8 summaries identify `rsi_divergence` as an existing setup but do not establish that its old setup outputs used anchor-aligned oscillator sampling.
- **Pivot-pair definition needs a deterministic contract.** The preregistration specifies regular divergence but does not fully state minimum/maximum pivot separation, whether to compare the immediately previous same-type pivot or search a bounded pair, treatment of intervening opposite pivots, minimum price/oscillator delta or numerical tolerance, duplicate extrema, or whether one confirmed pivot can emit multiple signals. These choices alter counts and must be fixed before any market scoring, not selected from results.
- **Confirmation-time signal and stop geometry must be explicit.** The stop references the latest confirmed divergence pivot, but a new pivot only becomes knowable after lag `L`; the implementation must use the correct pivot anchor value and confirmation-time ATR without shifting either. If confirmation occurs after price has crossed the proposed stop, classify invalidated-before-entry conservatively rather than manufacturing a trade.
- **4h alignment is a likely lookahead trap.** Generic resampling/forward-fill can attach the close of a still-open 4h bucket to its lower bars. Build a closed-candle availability timestamp explicitly, align by availability rather than bucket label, and fail closed on missing 4h inputs. EMA initialization/warm-up must be stable across full-history versus prefix computation.
- **“Next executable” is underspecified for gaps and stop-entry fills.** Next-bar open is deterministic only when the next bar exists and is valid. Stop-entry after signal high/low needs explicit gap-through fill, trigger ordering, expiry inclusivity, and no-entry treatment. Signal-bar barriers and invalidated-before-entry cannot be evaluated as if a position existed.
- **Intrabar OHLC cannot prove barrier order.** Minute paths help only when minute data are complete; if target and stop are both touched in one minute, stop-first is mandatory. Missing minute slots, duplicates, archived rejected rows, contract transitions, or timestamp misalignment require unresolved/fail-closed handling. A 1m strategy cannot use coarser OHLC to resolve same-bar order.
- **Costs are not fully observed by the frozen inputs.** The 0.10% round-trip fee and adverse 2x-slippage stress are specified, but the latter is a stress convention, not measured execution slippage. No order-book/depth or realtime data are authorized. Report base fee and stress separately; do not imply fillability or net live performance.
- **Universe/history and compute feasibility remain separate gates.** Archive availability does not prove point-in-time listing/delisting or contract-incarnation identity. Five 504/252 calendar-day folds with 4h warm-up require at least 1,764 usable days; per-symbol gaps can invalidate this even if the database span is long. Audit candle-level completeness, duplicates, symbol eligibility, and immutable data fingerprint before selecting the research window.
- **Dependence and promotion are easy to overstate.** All-emitted overlapping M2 is the primary denominator; non-overlap is only an eligibility gate. Direction accuracy is not target-first precision or profitability. The 1,000 permutation MDD is sequence risk only. Neighbor pass rate and M2 point estimate do not replace the synthetic-calibrated paired-baseline inference and frozen SPEC gates. Keep all outputs development-only unless sealed/forward provenance is independently established.
- **R018 is a hard access gate, not strategy evidence.** Even a completed synthetic calibration validates only its tested inference DGPs. The recorded plan separately requires B/C persistence/overlap and missingness checks and an OHLC-path method-only test for entry, barrier ordering, and costs. None of those results should be inferred from a Profile-A artifact or smoke; do not open/score market labels until every required gate is complete and independently reviewed.
