# Round 5 — crypto historical source feasibility (2026-11-16)

## Verdict

Binance Vision now has a checksum-verified **development-only** 1m sample for the fixed 86-symbol seed cohort through 2026-10. It does **not** qualify as a confirmatory OOS or prospective manual-signal test. No strategy outcomes were calculated from this backfill. The exact coverage, eight quarantined source months, and unresolved universe/gap classifications are recorded below.

## User-fixed outcome contract

The primary criterion is target-first precision ≥80% on at least 200 non-overlapping OOS events per claim. A target counts only if it is touched before stop/invalidation and before the registered deadline. Deadline-close direction accuracy is a separate secondary metric. For the MTF protocol, additional gates remain: at least 10% coverage, at least 20 symbols, clustered Wilson 95% lower bound above registered baselines, and positive net expectancy after costs. Ten-bar and twenty-bar horizons remain separate claims.

The underlying `chart_predictor/SPEC.md` had stale language saying the accuracy definition still awaited user confirmation. Section 5 now records the user's actual target-first definition and execution labels; earlier 75% tests remain historical experiments and are not retroactively reclassified. The 10% coverage and other MTF conditions are expressly additional protocol constraints, not replacements for the user's 80% target-first criterion.

## Archive metadata observations

The initial public-index observations below read only object metadata or directory names. The later backfill audit retrieved historical candle rows; neither step used API credentials or made account/trading requests.

### Initial 2021-01 seed sample (2026-11-16)

- Downloaded the Binance Vision USD-M monthly 1m kline archives for the fixed 2021-01 cohort of 86 symbols found in the metadata inventory. This is historical research data only; no Binance REST/API key, account, order endpoint, or live signal path was used.
- SQLite contains 86 manifest rows and 3,645,000 candle rows. The sum of manifest row counts equals the candle count, every candle maps to a manifest row, and `PRAGMA integrity_check` returned `ok`.
- A per-symbol timestamp-delta audit found zero missing minute intervals between each symbol's first observed bar and 2021-01-31 23:59 UTC. Eighty symbols have a full 44,640 rows; six start later in January and therefore have fewer rows. Missing minutes were not synthesized. This within-file continuity does not classify the pre-first-bar period as pre-listing, no-trade, or archive omission; that requires dated contract-status evidence.
- Two names, `DEFIUSDT` and `DOTECOUSDT`, are composite index contracts rather than single-coin underlyings. Excluding those leaves 84 candidate coin-underlying symbols, not a verified complete historical universe. Binance identifies [DEFI](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/ebee6da5df3946519fdb9ab27706d1a4) and [DOTECO](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/9381a09f6dae4899b8c00046a4afc08b) as composite baskets, not individual tokens.
- The sample is development-only and cannot support a sealed OOS claim: its performance history has not been tested, and the fixed cohort itself was chosen from archive-file availability rather than an independently proven historical listing census. No signal labels, strategy returns, hit rates, or parameters were calculated from this download.

### Full historical backfill and integrity audit (2026-11-17)

- The fixed seed cohort was backfilled from 2021-01 through 2026-10 where monthly archives were available. The local SQLite database contains **5,522 accepted symbol-month archives** for 86 symbols and **241,389,061 candle rows**. Manifest row counts sum to exactly 241,389,061; a month-by-month indexed range-count comparison found **0 manifest/candle row-count mismatches**. The candle table's `(symbol, open_ms)` primary key prevents duplicate keys, and `PRAGMA integrity_check` returned `ok`.
- Eight checksum-verified monthly source archives were rejected and quarantined rather than repaired: DOTUSDT 2023-11, EOSUSDT 2023-11, LRCUSDT 2023-09, ONTUSDT 2023-09, QTUMUSDT 2023-09, TRBUSDT 2023-09, XRPUSDT 2023-11, and ZENUSDT 2023-11. All eight are absent from `archive_manifest` and contribute **zero candle rows**. Each raw archive was re-downloaded and fully audited: each contains 43,200 ordered 1m rows, zero missing intervals, and exactly one taker-buy volume invariant violation. Seven violations are `taker_buy_base > volume`; LRCUSDT 2023-09 is `taker_buy_quote > quote_volume`. No source values were altered. The 2023-09 errors share a timestamp/row cluster across symbols; the 2023-11 errors also recur at matching row positions, consistent with a source-field anomaly but not proof of its cause.
- The fixed panel still has **84 single-coin-underlying candidates** after excluding the two composite index symbols `DEFIUSDT` and `DOTECOUSDT`. It is not a complete point-in-time historical contract universe. Archive availability and early termination alone do not classify a period as pre-listing, inactive/no-trade, delisted, or missing archive. The downloader's returned `missing_months: 0` applies only to the final resumed subset's unrecorded requests; it does not establish lifetime completeness of the full panel.
- An independent full-database timestamp-delta scan found **74 gaps spanning 577,125 missing minute slots across 38 of the 86 symbols**; the largest single gap is 43,200 minutes. The eight quarantined months explain 345,600 slots, so further gaps remain. This arithmetic detects discontinuities only: it cannot classify the remaining slots as pre-listing, no-trade, exchange outage, delisting, or archive omission. Until dated contract-status evidence resolves them, any strategy needing those intervals must treat affected windows as ineligible rather than fill or impute bars.
- The SQLite database is local and git-ignored; the large raw dataset was not committed. This download is permitted only for personal historical backtesting under the user's consent. Binance Vision-derived outputs are not authorized as a real-time or manual-trading signal feed.
- No strategy labels, TP/stop outcomes, returns, hit rates, or tuned parameters were calculated from the backfill. All historical bars are exposed development data, not a sealed OOS sample. The data-quality audit alone cannot satisfy the user's target-first ≥80% / OOS N≥200 contract.

### Synchronized monthly-cohort and auxiliary-data feasibility (2026-11-17)

- An aggregate-only query checked each accepted symbol-month manifest against the exact expected number of minute slots in that UTC month. The downloader validates each accepted row is in-month, minute-aligned, strictly ordered, and unique; manifest/candle counts were previously reconciled with zero mismatches. **37 of the 86 seed symbols have complete monthly archives in all 70 months from 2021-01 through 2026-10.** Across each calendar month, 51–84 seed symbols have full-month archive counts; thus a fixed ≥20-symbol *price-history subset exists structurally*. This corrects the earlier R024/RREADME statement that the archive had no synchronized 20-symbol history.
- This is only a structural OHLCV coverage finding, not a confirmed eligible contract cohort: the seed was selected from archive availability; current listing/contract status at each timestamp and inactive/delisted-contract coverage are not mapped. The remaining 49 symbols are not complete in every one of the 70 months. The 577,125 observed missing-minute slots across 38 symbols remain unclassified. Do not generalize from the 37-symbol survivor-conditioned subset as a complete historical universe.
- Binance's official `binance-public-data` helper documentation says USD-M/COIN-M historical `markPriceKlines`, `indexPriceKlines`, and `premiumPriceKlines` are downloadable from 2020 onward. Read-only HTTP HEAD probes returned 200 for BTCUSDT 1m monthly mark-price files in 2021-01 (1,239,482 B) and 2026-10 (1,024,247 B), and for the 2021-01 index-price (1,308,646 B) and premium-index (924,975 B) files. Index- and premium-series files for 2026-10 were not probed. These are object-availability checks only: no auxiliary archive body was downloaded or parsed, and no per-symbol panel coverage was established.
- A HEAD probe for one BTCUSDT 2021-01 USD-M monthly `aggTrades` archive returned 200 and 917,138,866 B. This confirms a possible historical trade-flow route but makes a broad all-symbol download materially expensive. No broad download was started. A guessed monthly `fundingRate` Vision path returned 404; that single path failure does not establish that no valid archive path exists. Funding coverage must be located and verified before use; do not silently substitute a rate API, mark-price proxy, or assumed cashflow.
- Four daily metrics archives (`BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT`, 2026-06-24 and 2026-06-25) and their publisher `.CHECKSUM` files were downloaded to `/private/tmp/vision_metrics_probe/`; all four SHA-256 checks passed. ZIP SHA-256: BTC 2026-06-24 `6bb5ab473189fa7c935b4d9a58d34b2c96cc329cd74fd6f9f17f8abc48ece649`; BTC 2026-06-25 `56e0751dabd8108d61d1f9de86931ac68e901dad202d4dbac03173e2768be68e`; ETH 2026-06-24 `1a6a493c12b7baf8274ea4acd79e8cb7ef655e0dc1d8ceed93626e2d93bdd688`; ETH 2026-06-25 `babb083bc28652856a247965e07b8faa3d05b69daa41ef9892f3ed6fbc7a8ba5`. Each CSV has 288 observations on a 00:00–23:55 UTC grid. Independent raw USD-M `aggTrades` archives for the same symbols/dates also passed publisher checksum verification. Reconstructed 5-minute taker buy/sell base-volume ratios (`is_buyer_maker=false` as taker buy) match the metrics ratio at the same `create_time` bin: per-day median relative error was 0.020–0.032% across the four symbol-days (288 matches/day); shifting the raw-trade bin by -5m or +5m raises median relative error to 29.9–34.4%. This falsifies a blanket five-minute shift for this taker-flow field on these two contracts and dates, including the 2026-06-25 boundary, but does not establish semantics for other metric fields, symbols, dates, or API conventions. The [public repository issue #491](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/491) remains a user-reported concern, not an official specification.
- Separately, the daily Vision open-interest values for BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT on 2026-11-01 were compared with all available 5-minute observations from Binance's public USD-M Open Interest Statistics REST endpoint (2 API requests, one per symbol). For each symbol, all 288 Vision values exactly matched the REST observation timestamped `create_time + 5 minutes` (max relative difference 0); matching the same timestamp instead had max relative error 0.8377% for BTC and 0.6305% for ETH. On this sample, `create_time` is therefore not a safe as-of availability timestamp for OI: using it directly would expose the value five minutes early. This conclusion is limited to two symbols on one date; verify more dates and metric fields before production research. The official [Open Interest Statistics API documentation](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Open-Interest-Statistics) limits history to the latest one month and calls the REST timestamp period-end.
- **Later timing audit, 2026-11-17 (R026; supersedes the initial single-day OI wording and adds the taker-field availability caveat):** across three dates and two symbols, 1,728 Vision OI rows matched the REST observation at `create_time + 5m` exactly; each date-symbol archive had 288 complete grid rows. The REST endpoint is another Binance publication path, not an independent vendor. The taker-flow bins from June 24/25 match the same labeled raw-trade interval best, but that interval is not complete until `create_time + 5m`; per-day median errors were 0.020–0.032%, with some >1% outliers. Only these fields/samples were checked. Other columns and broader historical availability remain unverified. See [`R026`](../chart_predictor/strategy_lab/runs/2026-11-17-r026-vision-metrics-timing/decision.md) and [`results`](../chart_predictor/strategy_lab/runs/2026-11-17-r026-vision-metrics-timing/results.json).
- **Updated gate:** the expanded Vision history can support a development screen on a survivor-conditioned, fully archived 37-symbol price panel after a candidate is newly preregistered. It still cannot establish the user-required confirmatory OOS result: no final access boundary is certified, the PIT historical contract universe is missing, the 37-symbol history is survivor-conditioned, and funding/executable-cost inputs remain unverified. Any strategy result from those bars must be labeled exploratory unless those independent gates are resolved.
