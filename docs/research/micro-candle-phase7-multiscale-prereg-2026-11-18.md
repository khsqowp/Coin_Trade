# Phase 7 preregistration — fixed micro-patterns at 3m and 5m scale

Status: protocol drafted after Phase 5 and 6 results. Outcome labels must remain unopened until two independent reviewers approve aggregation, timing, and fold-boundary semantics. This is a scale-replication study, not a new strategy search.

## Question

Do the two already-frozen 1-minute candle rules retain target-first behavior when the same candle definitions are applied to UTC-aligned 3-minute and 5-minute candles? This expands observation scale without inventing thresholds or adding a macro filter.

## Frozen input and scope

- Use only `chart_predictor/data/candles.sqlite`, the previously pinned 100-symbol/1-minute snapshot: 1,864,249,344 bytes, SHA-256 `cac23a3d18be66e226bdf8c3dbc0d08f5c6c185d4f18ed2a8687f08d2b6f67b1`, completed through `2026-11-14T00:29:00Z`.
- Open SQLite read-only; fail if the database hash/size changes, a sidecar exists, or its schema/symbol count differs. The database audit recorded 17,142,161 rows across 100 symbols with no missing in-span minutes, invalid OHLCV, off-grid timestamps, null rows, or duplicate keys.
- Construct each N-minute candle using `bucket_start = floor(1m_open_ms / (N*60,000)) * (N*60,000)`. Require exactly N valid constituent rows with timestamps `bucket_start + j*60,000` for every `j=0…N-1`; aggregate open=first open, high=max high, low=min low, close=last close. Its availability time is `bucket_start + N*60,000`. Incomplete, malformed, off-grid, or non-contiguous groups break aggregation; never forward-fill. A signal is eligible only when its full aggregate candle is available at or before the fixed completed-candle cutoff. Partial terminal groups are never signals.
- Use the same exact five UTC folds as Phase 5, with half-open `[start,end)` membership, intentionally assigning both scaled rules by aggregate availability time (the Phase 6 convention; this intentionally differs from Phase 5's original anchor-in-fold membership): F1 `[2026-07-16T18:10:00Z, 2026-08-10T00:00:00Z)`, F2 `[2026-08-10T00:00:00Z, 2026-09-03T00:00:00Z)`, F3 `[2026-09-03T00:00:00Z, 2026-09-27T00:00:00Z)`, F4 `[2026-09-27T00:00:00Z, 2026-10-21T00:00:00Z)`, F5 `[2026-10-21T00:00:00Z, 2026-11-14T00:29:00Z)`. A signal available exactly at a boundary belongs to the fold beginning at that boundary. A preceding completed aggregate bar may be used as known feature history. Outcomes may not cross the assigned fold end.
- This archive and its calendar blocks have already been exposed to other strategy research. They are development diagnostics, not independent OOS confirmation.

## Frozen signal rules

Test only these two existing rule families, with no parameter changes:

1. **Body dominance (Phase 5):** emit LONG iff `close>open` and `abs(close-open)/(high-low) >= 0.80`; SHORT iff `close<open` and the same fraction is at least 0.80. Doji and zero-range bars do not signal. The rule itself does not require ATR, so a valid body-dominance signal remains issued even if its later ATR/path is unavailable.
2. **Two-candle sweep/reclaim (Phase 6):** on adjacent aggregate bars `bar1`, `bar2`, LONG iff `low2<low1`, `high2<=high1`, and `(high1+low1)/2 < close2 < high1`; SHORT iff `high2>high1`, `low2>=low1`, and `low1<close2<(high1+low1)/2`. Sweeping both extremes or any invalid/non-adjacent pair emits no signal.

No price-regime, BTC trend, volume, indicator, session, or cross-asset filter is allowed. Do not optimize thresholds, invert a failed side, or add candidate rules after opening outcomes.

## Entry, risk, and outcome

- Decide only after the signal aggregate bar closes; enter at the next contiguous aggregate bar open.
- Body-dominance stop distance is the causal ATR(14) simple mean of aggregate true range, `TR=max(high-low, abs(high-prior_close), abs(low-prior_close))`, using data through the signal bar only. ATR is available only when all 14 current/prior-close inputs are valid, contiguous aggregate bars. A body signal without ready ATR remains issued and its path is unresolved, not dropped. Stop and target are each 1R from entry.
- Sweep/reclaim stop is the signal's `bar2` extreme; target is 1R from the next aggregate open using the positive entry-to-stop risk.
- For sweep/reclaim only, if the next open is at/beyond its pre-entry bar2 stop (`LONG: entry_open <= bar2.low`; `SHORT: entry_open >= bar2.high`), retain the emitted signal as a resolved no-trade failure: it contributes zero target-first success and zero PnL, with no fee. Report no-trade count separately; exclude undefined-R no-trades from mean net R and disclose that denominator. For body dominance, ATR stop/target are both defined from the entry open; a valid positive ATR and valid entry are tradable. Missing/invalid ATR or entry/path data leaves the signal unresolved in the all-issued denominator; do not label it a no-trade.
- Test 10 and 20 aggregate bars independently: 3m => 30m/60m; 5m => 50m/100m. Begin path checks with the entry bar. Stop wins same-bar stop/target ties. A stop gap fills at the adverse open; a target fills at the target barrier. Missing, malformed, discontinuous, cutoff-crossing, or fold-crossing paths remain unresolved failures in all-issued M2.
- Round-trip cost fractions are `0.0014` base and `0.0028` stress. Let `side=+1` for LONG and `side=-1` for SHORT, `entry_price` be the next aggregate open, and `stop_price` be the rule's stop. After choosing the exit, compute `gross_return = side * (exit_price / entry_price - 1)`, `risk_fraction = abs(entry_price - stop_price) / entry_price`, and `net_R = (gross_return - round_trip_cost_fraction) / risk_fraction`. On target-first/stop-first, fill at the barrier except adverse-open stop gaps; if neither barrier is reached, exit at the close of the Hth outcome bar. Binary target-first M2 itself is not cost-adjusted. Funding is not modeled. Unresolved paths and no-trades count as zero successes in all-issued M2.
- Report all-issued M2 = target-first successes / all issued signals; separately report resolved tradable target-first and its denominator, unresolved/path resolution, no-trades, direction at deadline, mean net R at both costs and its resolved-tradable denominator, non-overlap results, and fold results.
- Controls use identical candidate anchors/horizons. For body dominance, both momentum and random controls use the candidate's same entry and signal-time ATR(14) risk distance, placing each control's stop and 1R target according to its own side. Deterministic random direction is SHA-256 with seed `20261118`, keyed `seed|tag|symbol|signal_close_ms`, where tag is `random`. The body-direction momentum control is identical to the candidate by construction; label it as an implementation identity check, not an independent baseline or superiority evidence. For sweep/reclaim, momentum direction is bar2 body direction (a flat bar uses the deterministic `momentum-zero` SHA side), and both it and random direction use their own next-open entry and the corresponding side's bar2 extreme as stop, deriving their own risk and 1R target. If a sweep/reclaim control's entry is at/beyond its own stop, retain that anchor in the control's all-anchor denominator as a resolved zero-success no-trade with zero PnL/no fee; report this count and exclude it only from that control's resolved-tradable mean-net-R denominator.

## Label-blind frequency gate

Before opening any future price path or scoring target/stop hits, report eligible complete aggregate anchors, signals by rule/side/timeframe/fold/symbol, coverage, and timestamp-based non-overlap counts for each horizon. Signals are assigned by availability time and remain counted if the next aggregate bar/path is missing, beyond the cutoff, or crosses the fold boundary. Per symbol, fold, rule, bar size, and horizon, select the earliest signal by availability timestamp, then skip signals until the prior H-bar outcome has elapsed. The H outcome bars are the next aggregate bar through the Hth bar, inclusive; a new signal exactly at the availability time of the previous Hth outcome bar is allowed, matching the prior Phase 5 boundary convention. Proceed independently for each cell only if that cell has at least 200 non-overlap issued signals across at least 20 symbols; a failed cell is not outcome-scored and cannot be rescued by pooling it with another cell. There is no arbitrary coverage threshold; publish coverage as a descriptive statistic.

## Decision rules

- A cell can only advance to human review if all apply: all-issued M2 >=80%; mean net R after 14bp >0 over resolved tradable events; candidate M2 is not below either matched control (body-dominance momentum identity is reported only as a plumbing check); path resolution >=95%; non-overlap N>=200 across >=20 symbols; and an independent scalar/vector audit has at least one resolved trade and zero label/no-trade mismatches with net-R error <1e-12. If no-trades exist, audit at least one and require zero mismatch.
- A data/implementation gate failure is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not a strategy rejection. A performance-gate failure after data gates pass rejects that exact rule/timeframe/horizon cell.
- No tuning or Monte Carlo rescue. Since multiple cells are evaluated, report every cell and do not select a winner as confirmatory evidence. Any survivor still requires a genuinely untouched prospective/OOS evaluation and dependence-aware inference before promotion.
- The repaired 2020–2026 `vision_dev_repaired.sqlite` is excluded from this target-first study: it contains six symbols and therefore cannot satisfy the already-fixed 20-symbol non-overlap gate. Do not weaken the gate to make that dataset pass.
