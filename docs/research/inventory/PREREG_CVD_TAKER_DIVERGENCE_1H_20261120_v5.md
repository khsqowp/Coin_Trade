# Preregistration v5 amendment: dependence-aware inference

> Superseded before outcome scoring by v6. Read v2–v6 as a version chain; v6 closes the remaining undefined-replicate handling. No candidate outcome has been inspected or calculated.

- Frozen: 2026-11-20, before outcomes.
- Scoring remains blocked until four independent reviewers approve the complete v2–v5 chain and the pre-outcome input/funding audit is accepted.
- The 10 inferential contrasts are fixed: candidate versus each of four direction baselines at 10h and 20h (8 contrasts), plus CVD versus price-only at 10h and 20h (2 contrasts). Do not add, drop, or select contrasts after observing results.

## Replace v2/v3/v4 inferential rules

1. **Point estimates and frozen legacy gate.** Report event-weighted M2 for every arm, horizon, and side. Preserve the frozen Wilson-95%-lower-bound-above-strongest-baseline-point gate for comparability, but label the Wilson interval as independence-assuming; it is not valid standalone evidence under the observed dependence structure. The dependence-aware simultaneous bounds below are an additional required inferential gate, not a replacement that weakens the frozen criterion.
2. **Dependence-aware resampling unit.** Use the fixed UTC daily grid and synchronized seven-calendar-day moving blocks exactly as v4 defines. A sampled block carries every event row for that calendar date, including all symbols and all treatment/control labels, preserving same-day market shocks, overlapping labels, and cross-arm pairing. The fixed grid retains dates with zero events. Do not treat event rows as independent.
3. **Bootstrap replicate count and PRNG.** Use exactly 10,000 replicates from NumPy `Generator(PCG64(20261120))`. Within each replicate, draw block starts uniformly with replacement from integers `0` through `K-7` inclusive, concatenate contiguous non-wrapping seven-day blocks until K dates are reached, and truncate to K. Use the same date-index sequence for all arms, horizons, sides, and contrasts in that replicate. Record implementation versions and the sampled indices hash.
4. **Absolute M2 uncertainty.** For candidate M2 at each horizon, calculate the event-weighted M2 on each replicate. The reported one-sided 95% lower bound is the empirical 5th percentile using `numpy.quantile(array, 0.05, method="linear")`. If a replicate has zero candidate events, it is undefined; do not redraw. If more than 1% of replicates are undefined at either horizon, that horizon is NOT EVALUABLE. These lower bounds are dependence-aware diagnostics but not an additional threshold beyond the frozen candidate point threshold M2>=80%.
5. **Paired direction-baseline contrasts.** On every treatment anchor, an abstaining momentum baseline, invalid entry, missing path, timeout, or non-target-first result is a miss. For each horizon and baseline, compute the event-weighted M2 difference `candidate - baseline` on the same sampled calendar dates. Baseline standalone coverage/M2 are also reported separately; flat is N/A and excluded. Report the observed paired difference and one-sided 95% lower percentile bound.
6. **CVD versus price-only contrasts.** At each horizon, calculate the event-weighted difference from the separately issued CVD and price-only streams on the same resampled dates. Preserve zero-event dates and do not manufacture paired trades. Report the observed difference and one-sided 95% lower percentile bound.
7. **Familywise control without miscalibrated p-values.** Do not calculate or report McNemar p-values or centered/uncentered bootstrap p-values. For each of the 10 fixed contrasts, report a simultaneous one-sided lower bound at quantile `alpha=0.05/10=0.005` (0.5th percentile, `numpy.quantile(array, 0.005, method="linear")`). This Bonferroni construction controls the family-wise error rate at 0.05 under valid marginal bootstrap coverage; disclose that coverage remains approximate for finite dependent samples. No cross-family claim is allowed outside these 10 predeclared contrasts.
8. **Decision rule.** A candidate horizon can be considered for further study only if (a) point M2>=80%, (b) the frozen Wilson 95% lower bound exceeds the strongest valid baseline point estimate, (c) simultaneous lower bounds for candidate-minus-each of the four directional baselines are >0, (d) the candidate's complete-path coverage and all original sample/symbol/cost/net-expectancy gates pass, and (e) no required-input integrity failure exists. A CVD incremental-edge claim requires simultaneous lower bounds for CVD-minus-price-only >0 at both horizons. These remain historical exploratory criteria only; they cannot confer core/OOS status.
9. **Resampling failure.** If any contrast has more than 1% undefined replicates, mark that contrast and dependent decision NOT EVALUABLE. Do not modify block length, grid, statistic, PRNG, or replicate count after outcomes.
10. **Non-overlap sample is fixed without realized outcomes.** Supersede v3 clause 9's exit-dependent greedy rule. Build the sorted union of entry timestamps across the CVD and price-only arms before reading any outcome. Treat all symbols at one timestamp as one cluster. Retain the earliest cluster, then retain the next cluster only when its entry timestamp is at least 20 hours after the previously retained cluster's entry timestamp; repeat. This fixed 20-hour spacing is based on the maximum scheduled horizon, not realized exit times, and is common to both arms and both horizons. For each arm/horizon report retained clusters that actually contain at least one signal, event-row N, and symbol count. The frozen non-overlap N>=200 applies to retained signal-bearing timestamp clusters, separately from all-issued N. Baseline comparisons use candidate treatment anchors and inherit the same retained-cluster membership. This selection uses only signal timestamps, never future prices/outcomes.
11. **Scheduled anchor eligibility and gaps.** Supersede v4's path-availability wording. Include an entry anchor if its scheduled 20th hourly candle close timestamp is strictly before `2025-03-01T00:00:00Z`; this decision uses timestamps only. Do not remove an otherwise time-eligible anchor because future bars are missing. Keep it as `DATA_GAP`, an M2 miss, and a required-data failure; do not read data at or beyond the development endpoint. Apply the same scheduled-time rule to every arm and baseline.

## Rationale and claim boundary

Wilson/McNemar independence assumptions do not hold for overlapping 10h/20h outcomes or synchronized crypto symbols. The original Wilson gate is retained exactly but cannot stand alone; calendar-block resampling adds dependence-aware simultaneous lower bounds. These avoid relying on raw p-values from an uncentered bootstrap distribution. This amendment does not solve survivorship, historical exposure, or source-timing limitations. No score from the exposed historical interval can qualify as OOS or a core.
