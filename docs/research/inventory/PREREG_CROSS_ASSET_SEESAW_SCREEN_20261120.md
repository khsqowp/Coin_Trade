# Preregistration: cross-asset crypto return seesaw screen

- Status: frozen before outcome calculation. One direction, one leader basket, two fixed horizons; no parameter search.
- Hypothesis: the equal-weight return of four large, locally available USD-M leaders (BTC, ETH, XRP, LTC) over the just-closed hour predicts the opposite next-hour direction of each other eligible coin. This is a simple, fixed proxy inspired by [Jia et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2023.101428), not their rolling LASSO replication. [Guo et al. (2024)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165188924000551) reports positive cross-coin spillovers using Binance data, so direction is uncertain and is tested rather than presumed.
- Signal: for each target coin other than BTC/ETH/XRP/LTC, after UTC hour t closes, compute the arithmetic mean of the four leaders' log returns from t-1 close to t close. If positive, target signal is SHORT; if negative, LONG; exact zero or any missing leader bar emits no signal. Entry is target's next hourly open. No same-bar data are used.
- Horizons: H=1 and H=4 hourly candles; both fixed in advance. H=4 anchors only at UTC hours divisible by four. Exit at the H-th held candle close. No barrier-based M2 is claimed because the source hypothesis supplies no stop/target. M2 remains NOT MEASURED; this screen is only a precondition for separate barrier-rule preregistration.
- Controls: same-anchor momentum (follow, rather than fade, the leader-basket sign), always-long, always-short, and no-trade. Controls are diagnostic, not additional tunable candidates.
- Costs: subtract fixed 10 bp and 30 bp round-trip fee proxies and add signed archived funding for the held interval. Spread/slippage are unavailable, so positive results remain incomplete execution proxies.
- Data: fixed 32 target symbols excluding the four leaders from the verified 36-symbol common USD-M cohort, 2023-03 through 2026-05. The historical periods were exposed in earlier research; all results are exploratory and cannot qualify as untouched OOS.
- Splits: development `[2023-03-23 15:00, 2025-03-01 00:00) UTC`, validation-exposed `[2025-03-01 00:00, 2025-11-01 00:00) UTC`, evaluation-previously-exposed `[2025-11-01 00:00, 2026-06-01 00:00) UTC`. All splits descriptive only.
- Dependence: calculate per-event means and synchronized UTC-hour cross-sectional means. Use a deterministic 7-day circular moving-block bootstrap with 1,000 draws on synchronized market-hour observations; confidence intervals are descriptive only.
- Screen: proceed to a separately preregistered triple-barrier M2 design only if at least one fixed horizon has positive synchronized net mean under both costs and positive lower 95% block-bootstrap bounds under both costs. Otherwise REJECT. This does not qualify as a core; final core gates remain the Coin_Trade specification, including M2 >=80%, >=200 non-overlapping signals, >=20 symbols, >=10% coverage, positive net expectancy, baseline comparison, and genuinely untouched forward evidence.
- Causality: mutate all target and leader values strictly after the signal close and require signal sides through the cutoff unchanged. Test exact zero, missing leader, entry timing, horizon indexing, cost/funding signs, and split boundary.
