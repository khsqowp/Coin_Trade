# Preregistration: USD-M funding-tail reversal

- Frozen before the run; one candidate, no parameter grid and no result-driven edits.
- Candidate source: independent read-only Phase 8 review. This is one bounded hypothesis, not a claim that all possible strategies were tested.
- Input: the pre-audited 36-symbol Binance USD-M 1h cohort, 2023-03-23 15:00 UTC through 2026-06-01 00:00 UTC. These dates and symbols were exposed by earlier research; all splits are exploratory, not untouched OOS.
- Funding source: archived `calc_time` and `last_funding_rate`; rate becomes eligible only at its recorded settlement timestamp. Trigger distribution uses strictly earlier events from the same symbol, over the preceding 180 calendar days, with at least 30 observations. Quantiles use NumPy linear interpolation. Current event is excluded from its own distribution.
- Signal: at an eligible funding event, rate strictly below the trailing 10th percentile emits LONG; rate strictly above the 90th percentile emits SHORT; otherwise no signal. Strict tails are required because funding rates often tie at zero; inclusive thresholds would mark an entire tied distribution as directional events. Only the first hourly candle whose open timestamp is strictly greater than `calc_time` is an entry candidate. A funding event is not treated as known before its archive timestamp.
- Risk: ATR(14), simple rolling mean of true range, from the last fully closed hourly candle before the signal timestamp. Entry is the next eligible candle open. Stop distance is 1 ATR; target distance is 2 ATR. No valid prior ATR or invalid stop at entry means issued signal is retained as an invalid-entry failure, not dropped.
- Barrier: inspect 10 consecutive hourly OHLC bars from entry. Stop first on same-bar ambiguity; adverse stop gaps fill at bar open; target gaps are credited at target; timeout exits at bar-10 close and is M2 failure. Exit path is evaluated only within its chronological split.
- Primary outcome: M2 target-first rate over every issued valid/invalid signal. Also report Wilson 95% interval, per-symbol and market-time non-overlap counts/rates, M1 entry-to-timeout direction accuracy, coverage, symbol distribution, exit reasons, gross/net return and R expectancy.
- Non-overlap: same symbol event intervals may not overlap for symbol N; market-wide N additionally deduplicates across all symbols by entry/expiration interval. Both counts are required; neither implies statistical independence across correlated assets.
- Baseline: frozen always-SHORT 1R-stop/2R-target/H10 rate, selected in development by the prior 38-setup study (0.17197935318319987 development mean, equal-weighted by setup). Report it without reselecting. Baseline is not an untouched control because prior study exposed these dates.
- Costs: base round-trip 10 bp, stress round-trip 30 bp, the previously documented legacy historical crypto assumptions. Apply archived signed funding while held. Spread and slippage are not modeled; no execution-realism claim.
- Splits: development 2023-03-23 15:00 to 2025-03-01; validation 2025-03-01 to 2025-11-01; sealed-label range 2025-11-01 to 2026-06-01. The last two are already exposed and must be labeled previously exposed exploratory evaluation, never final sealed validation.
- Pass gates remain fixed: M2 >=80%, Wilson lower above frozen baseline, per-symbol non-overlap N>=200, market-wide non-overlap N>=200 reported separately, >=20 symbols, coverage >=10%, positive net expectancy under both cost cases. Failure of any gate is REJECT; insufficient source integrity is BLOCKED. Even meeting all numeric gates cannot earn final approval because this cohort has been exposed and slippage is absent.
- Causality tests: prefix invariance and future mutation must show that changing future funding/OHLC does not alter earlier signal timestamps, sides, thresholds, or ATR. Test adversarial future-only changes. Archive hash/timestamp audit must pass before scoring.
- Reproducibility: persist this file's SHA-256, source archive fingerprint, code hashes, exact signals/outcomes, run metadata, and a concise report in a new versioned directory. Never overwrite an earlier run.
