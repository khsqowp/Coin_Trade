# Preregistration v3 amendment: 1h taker-volume CVD divergence

> Superseded before outcome scoring by v5. Read v2–v5 as a version chain; v5 overrides the non-overlap, anchor-eligibility, and inference rules. No candidate outcome has been inspected or calculated.

- Frozen: 2026-11-20, before outcome scoring.
- Purpose: close the independent-review objections on evaluation boundaries, baseline abstention, multiplicity, overlap, and strict missing-data treatment. This is still an exploratory historical study, not sealed OOS or a core-approval test.
- Gate: four independent roles (Blue, Red, Methods, Judge) must review this amendment together with v2; Red and Methods must identify no unresolved fatal scoring ambiguity. Input QA must pass. Only then may a scorer be implemented and outcome scoring begin.

## Frozen clarifications and amendments to v2

1. **Common complete-path anchor grid.** Both 10h and 20h arms use exactly the same entry anchors. An anchor is eligible only if its next-open entry is at or after `2023-03-23T00:00:00Z` and the complete 20 hourly-bar outcome path ends strictly before `2025-03-01T00:00:00Z`. No bar at or after the development interval end may be read for features or labels. This rule applies before signal generation and to every treatment, ablation, and baseline. Count and disclose excluded boundary anchors without outcome inspection.
2. **Fixed deterministic direction baseline.** Baseline (d) hashes the UTF-8 bytes of the exact string `20261120|{symbol}|{entry_open_ms}` with SHA-256. If the least-significant bit of the first digest byte is 0, side is long; if 1, short. No language-specific hash function is allowed.
3. **Symmetric baseline barriers.** For every direction baseline at a CVD-treatment anchor, use that anchor's same entry and absolute initial-risk distance `D = abs(entry - candidate_stop)`. For side sign `s` (+1 long, -1 short), baseline stop is `entry - s*D` and target is `entry + s*D`. Baseline outcomes use the same OHLC ordering, horizons, costs, and funding rules as the candidate. Invalid/gapped anchors stay in the denominator as misses.
4. **Momentum abstention.** Baseline (c) uses the close-to-close return ending at the second pivot's availability bar `i2+3`; positive means long, negative means short, exact zero means abstain. For standalone baseline M2, report M2 over issued trades and separately report coverage. For paired comparisons on every candidate anchor, an abstention is a miss, not a removed pair. A zero-coverage flat baseline is M2=N/A and cannot be the comparator.
5. **Funding event timestamp.** Interpret archive `calc_time` as settlement time, matching the official USD-M `fundingTime` field. The audit record must include the external read-only cross-check method, symbols/dates sampled, exact timestamp/rate match counts, and response hashes. A settlement exactly at `entry_open_ms` is excluded; include only `funding_time > entry_open_ms` and `funding_time <= exit_bar_close_ms`. If that source mapping fails, price-only outcome analysis may proceed but all funding-adjusted expectancy is NOT EVALUABLE and the candidate cannot pass any gate.
6. **Conservative funding at exit candle.** Settlements strictly before the exit candle are charged signed cashflow `s*rate` (payment positive, credit negative). A settlement inside the exit candle is charged only `max(s*rate, 0)` because intrabar exit order is unknown; report actual signed funding separately and run the v2 sensitivity excluding settlements in the exit candle. No missing event or interval is silently treated as zero.
7. **Missing data is fail-closed.** Required-field, path-bar, and funding-interval failures are retained as issued observations where applicable and counted as M2 misses; they are not removed from coverage or denominators. Any affected performance cell with incomplete required inputs is NOT EVALUABLE and cannot pass. No filling or interpolation is permitted.
8. **First-month partial archive.** Only the first month may be left-truncated. It is eligible for input QA if every observed bar from its first timestamp to the final hour of that month is consecutive, and the exact leading unobserved-hour count is reported. This is not assumed to mean pre-listing. ATR warmup begins at the first available row. Every later month must be complete. The 2023-03-23 cutoff remains enforced at feature/signal eligibility.
9. **Market-shock cluster non-overlap.** For each arm and horizon, group all symbol events with the same `entry_open_ms` as one market-shock cluster. Process clusters in ascending entry time. Retain a cluster only if its timestamp is strictly later than the maximum exit-bar close among all events in the previously retained cluster; otherwise drop the entire cluster. Do not choose a symbol within a same-time cluster. Report retained cluster N, event-row N, symbols, and UTC-day counts. Future core N>=200 applies to independent retained time clusters and separately to event rows; symbol and day dependence must still be shown.
10. **Direction-baseline multiplicity.** The confirmatory exploratory family comprises the four directional baselines × two horizons = eight one-sided exact McNemar tests, paired on candidate treatment anchors. A baseline abstention or invalid/gap outcome is a miss. Apply Holm-Bonferroni across all eight p-values at family-wise alpha 0.05. Additionally, for either horizon to advance, the candidate Wilson 95% lower bound must exceed the maximum point M2 among baselines with coverage at least equal to candidate treatment coverage. Always-long, always-short and deterministic SHA baseline have full coverage; zero-coverage flat is excluded. No baseline is selected after results are seen.
11. **CVD versus price-only increment.** This is the primary mechanism comparison, not a substitute for the frozen absolute M2 gate. At each horizon, use a synchronized 7-calendar-day moving-block bootstrap over UTC entry-date blocks, 1,000 replicates, seed `20261120`, and paired calendar blocks for CVD and price-only arms. The statistic is `M2_CVD - M2_price_only`; event absence on a date is preserved as absence, never synthesized. Apply Holm-Bonferroni across the two horizons, one-sided alpha 0.05. Report point difference, percentile interval, adjusted p-values, raw and adjusted decisions. A positive incremental claim requires both horizons' adjusted lower confidence bounds > 0; otherwise no incremental edge is claimed. If a fixed block cannot be formed or a horizon has fewer than 200 eligible date blocks/events, mark the test underpowered and do not replace the method after seeing outcomes.
12. **Coverage and denominator.** Candidate coverage denominator is all complete, eligible hourly symbol-bars in the fixed cohort after ATR warmup and the strict common anchor cutoff. Required-field failures are visible as data-gate failures and cannot shrink this denominator. Report issued signals, valid entries, invalid entries, missing-path labels, and evaluable outcomes separately. Coverage is issued directional events divided by the fixed eligible-bar denominator; abstentions are not signals.

## Unchanged v2 gates and restrictions

- Keep v2's exact universe, divergence/pivot/confirmation definitions, contract OHLC barriers, 1R target, 10h/20h horizons, stop-first ambiguity, fee proxies, funding stress, M1/M2 definitions, and no-parameter-search rule.
- Both horizons must be reported; no post-hoc best horizon or side. Report long, short, and combined streams.
- Historical development data were previously exposed. Even a favorable result is reject/debug evidence only and cannot establish OOS, core approval, deployment, or live/manual signals.
- Phase 9 remains blocked unless a future immutable core gate is passed on genuinely untouched forward data and Phase 7 robustness.

Preserve per-signal records and reproducibility hashes. Do not publish only a selected winner.
