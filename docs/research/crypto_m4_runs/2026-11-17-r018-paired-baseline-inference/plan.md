# R018 — paired-baseline M2 inference calibration

Status: synthetic-only method study preregistered and cleared for execution after independent red-team review. No market candles, strategy outcomes, or sealed/forward labels are inputs. It cannot qualify a strategy or authorize opening market outcomes.

## Purpose and correction to R016/R017

The user-fixed crypto success rule is a target-first point estimate (M2) of at least 80%; deadline-close direction accuracy is reported separately. The existing SPEC also requires a dependence-aware 95% lower bound to exceed registered baselines. R016 and R017 instead calibrated tests of the stronger null `candidate M2 = 0.80` and promotion only when a lower confidence bound exceeded 0.80. Those are valid stress results for those candidate methods, but they do not directly validate the actual candidate-versus-baseline acceptance estimand. R018 addresses that gap and does not erase either rejected result.

## Frozen estimands

- One claim is one timeframe × one forecast deadline (10 or 20 bars). The full family has 24 claims. All claims are evaluated jointly; no post-result timeframe or deadline selection is allowed.
- The user explicitly fixed the denominator: **M2 is calculated over all emitted forecasts with resolved labels, including overlapping forecasts.** The claim-level outcome-blind non-overlap sample is a separate eligibility gate and must contain N≥200 across at least 20 symbols. It is never substituted for the all-emitted M2 denominator.
- The N/sample rule is exact: for each claim and symbol, scan emitted anchors chronologically; keep the earliest event, then suppress later events until the kept event's registered deadline (10T or 20T) has passed. Apply no outcome, target, stop, confidence, or realized-coverage information during selection. The resulting union must include at least 200 events and all 20 frozen synthetic symbols. This subset is used **only** for N/symbol eligibility; neither M2 nor candidate-minus-baseline contrasts use it.
- Primary point gate: candidate M2 ≥0.80. M2 counts `TARGET_FIRST` as 1; `STOP_FIRST`, `INVALIDATED_BEFORE_ENTRY`, and fully observed `TIMEOUT_NO_TARGET` as 0. `UNRESOLVED_DATA` makes the whole claim `INCONCLUSIVE` under this registered screen. The 10-bar and 20-bar deadlines remain distinct claims.
- Coverage is emitted forecast IDs divided by all scheduled frozen-cohort symbol × completed-T-bar opportunities, per timeframe and overall. No-signal/neutral/abstain opportunities remain in the denominator; synthetic feed availability is 100%. Report issue rate, resolved-label rate, and coverage separately. An ineligible claim cannot be promoted and cannot be silently omitted from family-error reporting.
- Candidate and signal baselines share the exact same emitted anchors. The momentum baseline direction is the sign of the prior completed T-bar close-to-close return; zero return falls back to the fixed SHA-256 random direction. The random baseline direction is one pre-generated SHA-256 bit keyed by run seed, symbol, timeframe, and anchor. For both baselines, use the same next-entry timestamp, same ex-ante candidate risk distance, same target/risk multiple and deadline; reconstruct the stop and target symmetrically around the baseline's direction-specific executable entry. Keep paired labels on identical anchors. A same-anchor comparator mechanically forced to copy the candidate is prohibited.
- `Always flat/no-trade` has no target-first precision denominator; its M2 is undefined, not zero. It is a zero-net-return policy comparator for a separate expectancy test. R018 does not calibrate that policy-P&L estimand.
- A method-level family promotion occurs only if the claim is eligible, candidate all-emitted M2 point estimate is at least 0.80, and simultaneous one-sided lower bounds for candidate-minus-each matched signal baseline are strictly positive. **M2 and both paired baseline contrasts use the exact same all-emitted, resolved events at the same anchors.** Report point precision and direction-at-deadline separately. Do not use the M2 lower bound above 0.80 as the user's gate. The simulation must separately report the joint-rule false-promotion rate; the paired-baseline FWER alone does not prove that the descriptive 80% point gate has 6% type-I error.

## Synthetic event generator

- 20 fixed symbols; 12 timeframe widths; separate 10-bar and 20-bar claims; 24 claims × two signal baselines in one complete family (48 paired contrasts). The all-emitted forecast ledger determines M2; an outcome-blind non-overlap selector determines only N≥200 and ≥20-symbol eligibility.
- Simulate 365 UTC days with 24 fixed hourly anchor opportunities per day for every symbol × timeframe stream. This is a computational surrogate, not the physical candle cadence of any timeframe; UTC-day clustering is nevertheless explicit. Each scheduled opportunity is retained in the coverage denominator. Issue probability uses the fixed Markov process: initial hot-state probability 0.12; hot→cold 0.22; cold→hot 0.035; issue probability hot 0.48/cold 0.045. No signal issuance may depend on any current/future synthetic outcome.
- Create paired Bernoulli target-first labels. For claim index `c=0..23`, set null marginal `p_c={0.25,0.50,0.80,0.95}[c mod 4]` and paired latent correlation `r_c={0.25,0.50,0.80}[c mod 3]` for each of its two baseline comparisons. Candidate and both baselines have exactly equal marginal `p_c` under the null. In profiles B/C, each latent factor is a stationary Gaussian AR(1), initialized `N(0,1)` with innovation variance `1−ρ²`; its future-window factor is the mean of samples at anchors `t+1…t+H`, standardized by the exact AR(1) mean variance. Set `M=sqrt(0.35)A+sqrt(0.45)B+sqrt(0.20)C`, where A is a shared UTC-time factor, B is symbol-specific, and C is timeframe-specific (shared by its 10/20-bar claims). Candidate and baseline latent scores are `Z=sqrt(r_c)M+sqrt(1−r_c)ε`, with independent standard-normal ε per forecast policy; apply threshold `Φ⁻¹(p_c)` to obtain the Bernoulli outcome. Profile A uses independent `M~N(0,1)` per event and no overlapping windows. Profile B sets all ρ=0 and uses overlapping 10/20-bin future windows. Profile C uses common ρ=0.70, symbol ρ=0.35, timeframe ρ=0.25 and overlapping 10/20-bin future windows. These are Bernoulli-label diagnostics, not price-path validation.
- Profiles are fixed: (A) **IID outcome factors** with the same Markov-clustered signal issuance specified above; (B) zero-AR shared-symbol/timeframe factors with overlapping future windows and Markov-clustered issuance; (C) the stated persistent/common-shock profile with overlapping windows and Markov-clustered issuance. “IID” in A refers only to independent per-event outcome factors, not IID signal issuance. Run all registered profiles; never select one by result. Baseline equality is the null for paired-superiority FWER. Add fixed alternatives `(p_candidate,p_baseline)∈{(0.79,0.50),(0.80,0.50),(0.80,0.80),(0.85,0.50),(0.85,0.80)}`; for claim index `c`, assign pair `c mod 5`. This gives five claims for each of the first four alternatives and four for the fifth. Apply the assigned baseline marginal `p_baseline` to **both** momentum and random baseline labels; use their same-anchor paired labels and the per-claim `r_c` dependence structure. Use the same mapping in every replication/profile; alternatives measure power and joint point-gate behavior and are not used to tune the method.
- Missingness stress uses two fixed rates: `q=0.01` independent Bernoulli missingness and 5% shock-conditional missingness, defined as unresolved when the absolute standardized shared UTC-time factor exceeds `Φ⁻¹(0.975)`. In line with the fail-closed prospective draft, any unresolved emitted event makes its claim `INCONCLUSIVE`; the claim cannot promote. Also report the conservative all-unresolved-as-loss M2 sensitivity. This is a synthetic decision-rule check, not final live recorder-quarantine authorization.

## Candidate inference procedure

Candidate: synchronized **one-way temporal cluster stationary bootstrap** of paired daily score vectors; inference is conditional on the 20-symbol frozen cohort, not a claim about a wider symbol population. For calendar day `d` and paired contrast `j`, let `n[d,j]` be the count of all emitted, resolved, matched events and `x[d,j]` the sum of `Y_candidate−Y_baseline`. Estimate `Δ[j]=Σx/Σn`; compute cluster-robust `SE[j]=sqrt(D/(D−1) × Σ(x[d,j]−Δ[j]n[d,j])²)/Σn[d,j]`, with `D=365` UTC days. For each bootstrap draw, sample D day indices by the circular stationary-bootstrap rule (uniform start; continue to the next UTC day with probability `1−1/L`, otherwise restart uniformly), resample the complete n/x vector for every contrast, and recompute `Δ*` and `SE*`. Compute `T*[j]=(Δ*[j]−Δ[j])/SE*[j]`; the draw statistic is the maximum T* across all eligible contrasts in the 48-contrast family. The 95th percentile of this max statistic is the shared critical value; simultaneous lower bound is `Δ[j]−c×SE[j]`. Family-wise paired-baseline false promotion is any eligible lower bound strictly above zero when the true paired Δ is nonpositive. This calibration is conditional on the frozen 20-symbol cohort, not a claim about a wider symbol population. The full candidate rule and any flat-baseline net-return inference are separate estimands.

Predeclared block lengths: 1, 2, 5, 10, and 20 **UTC calendar days**. This is a diagnostic range, not an invitation to choose the favorable result. A method is rejected if any registered equality-null DGP/block length fails its declared paired-comparison gate or has runtime/replication errors. Report the joint user-rule false-promotion rate separately; a point-estimate gate alone is not a 5% hypothesis test. Passing this stage permits only further synthetic price-path, barrier-tie, cost, missingness, and power validation; it never authorizes market-label scoring.

## Calibration gates and run order

1. Unit/invariant checks: future-only outcomes; outcome-blind non-overlap selection; exact same-anchor pairing; 10/20-bar claims; N≥200 and all 20 symbols per claim; per-timeframe coverage≥10%; coverage denominator includes every scheduled opportunity; max-T family includes all 48 contrasts; injected future peeking fails the causal guard.
2. Smoke run only: 10 synthetic replications, 199 bootstrap draws, fixed seed `20261120`; verify output schema, deterministic replay, zero runtime errors, and eligibility bookkeeping. This run is not used to assess or choose the method.
3. Independent Bernoulli boundary calibration under equal candidate/baseline rates (p assigned from the fixed set above): paired-superiority FWER must have a 95% Wilson upper bound ≤6% for each registered day-block length, with zero failed replications. At least 95% of replications must have all 24 claims eligible; otherwise the profile is `INCONCLUSIVE` and cannot pass by counting ineligible replications as non-promotions. Use 5,000 independent panels and 1,999 synchronized bootstrap draws per panel; master seed `20261123`, NumPy `SeedSequence` child seeds indexed by profile, panel, block length, and bootstrap. Reuse each panel's outcomes for every block length; never rerun only a favorable cell. The maximum-test quantile uses NumPy `method='higher'`. Report (i) paired-superiority FWER under equality, (ii) joint user-rule promotion rate under null and sub-80 alternatives, and (iii) confidence-bound coverage. The ≤6% gate applies to paired-baseline false superiority, not to the user's point-estimate rule; do not represent it as a ≤6% full-rule false-pass guarantee if the latter does not meet that rate.
4. Only if step 3 passes, evaluate the fixed overlap/persistence and missingness profiles with the same method and day blocks. Ineligible replications count as no promotion in unconditional FWER; also report FWER conditional on all 24 claims meeting eligibility. If the all-eligible replication rate is below 95%, classify that profile `INCONCLUSIVE`, not pass.
5. A separate method-only OHLC path simulation is required to validate barrier ordering, same-bar stop-first, executable entry, and base/stress costs before any market outcomes. If the paired-comparison method fails at any required profile, reject it and register a distinct iteration; do not tune its block lengths or thresholds using market results.

## Limitations and non-goals

- Synthetic Bernoulli outcomes do not represent OHLC barrier paths, target/stop order, fee drag, funding, spread, or liquidation. A calibration pass here would be necessary but not sufficient.
- The stated 80% is a point-estimate gate; paired-comparison calibration tests baseline superiority, not `candidate > 80%`. Empirically report how often the entire user rule promotes candidates under `(p_candidate,p_baseline)=(0.79,0.50)`; do not misstate that descriptive point-estimate rule as a ≤6% test of true p≥0.80.
- The user has fixed all-emitted M2 with a separate non-overlap N≥200 gate. R018 fixes synthetic comparator rules, coverage accounting, and unresolved handling for method calibration only. The final market protocol still needs independent approval of the executable baseline geometry, live data validity/coverage rule, cost treatment, recorder resolution policy, and dependence-aware inference.
- This run does not test a strategy, claim performance, access historical outcome labels, alter the user's threshold, or authorize live trading, account access, deployment, or data purchase.
