# R019-P alternatives, partial-null, and missingness preregistration

Status: **registered before R19-P implementation/run; no R19-P output or market labels have been opened.** R019-N A/B/C null results are fixed and recorded separately.

## Fixed design

- Use the exact R018 synthetic panel generator and R019 overlapping block-multiplier inference/intersection rule; do not alter either based on R019-N rates.
- Fresh master seed `20261125`; derive panel and bootstrap child seeds by `SeedSequence` from `(20261125, 20, scenario_index, profile_index, panel_index, block_length)`. Scenario indices: alternatives `1`, independent-1%-missingness `2`, common-shock-5%-missingness `3`.
- Each applicable scenario/profile cell: 5,000 independent panels, 1,999 draws for each of five block lengths `{1,2,5,10,20}`. Profiles A/B/C retain R018 definitions. `shock_5pct` is applicable only to B/C; A is structurally inapplicable because it has no common factor.
- Run cells: alternatives/no-missingness A/B/C; null/independent-1%-missingness A/B/C; null/common-shock-5%-missingness B/C. No other cells or profile substitutions.
- Keep the full 48-contrast eligible max family in every multiplier draw. A claim is a paired rejection only when both of its baseline contrasts have positive lower bounds at all five lengths. Full promotion adds eligibility and resolved `M2≥0.80`.

## Primary partial-null gate

In the alternatives panels, the fixed `(candidate p, baseline p)=(0.80,0.80)` group (`claim_index % 5 == 2`) has true paired effect zero; all other fixed alternative groups remain in the max family. For each panel, count a partial-null family error once if any eligible claim in that true-null group passes the paired two-baseline/all-scale rule. Separately count any full-rule promotion among that group. Report both rates with 95% Wilson intervals, using all 5,000 panels as denominator and treating ineligible/non-computable panels as non-promotions while reporting them separately.

Proceed beyond R19-P only if, in every A/B/C alternative profile, (a) partial-null paired FWER Wilson upper bound ≤6%, (b) true-null full-rule false-promotion Wilson upper bound ≤6%, (c) all-claim eligibility is ≥95%, and (d) there are zero numerical/runtime errors. Otherwise reject the method for market-label access. Report power by each of the five fixed alternative pairs, both all-emitted point-M2 gate rate and multiscale paired/full-rule promotion rate, conditional on eligible claims; these rates are diagnostics and may not be tuned into a new threshold.

## Missingness checks

- For the six fixed missingness cells, report eligible-claim distribution, unresolved counts, resolved-only M2, all-unresolved-as-loss M2, any promotion, paired coverage, and errors. No unresolved claim may be silently treated as eligible or as a resolved win. No strategy/power claim is made from these stress-only cells.
- These cells are robustness diagnostics, not a new promotion route. Any promotion with an unresolved claim is an implementation failure and rejects R019.
