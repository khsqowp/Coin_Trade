# R012 decision — draft, performance gate closed

The original R005 cannot pass its own coverage gate: R007 proves the full-20T suppression caps coverage near 5%. R012 proposes a new pre-label version with independent timeframe forecast ledgers and overlapping labels, while preserving the R005 mechanism and 108-cell grid. This can remove the structural cap; it cannot establish the resulting predictor will achieve 10% coverage or 80% accuracy.

Four reviewers examined the R012 design or bounded criteria. They found the original macro-only comparator tautological, and found the proposed replacement still mixes policy performance with signal-selection claims. Validation-frozen coverage thresholds cannot ensure equal test coverage or anchor composition. Synchronous calendar blocks do not create paired observations for policies with different anchors. The proposed composite p-value and confirmatory Holm language are withdrawn because the test statistics, variance/studentization, null-centering and validity checks are not frozen. Three reviewers worked from supplied design summaries rather than directly opening the local file, so this is not a full document audit.

Decision: `blocked_protocol_draft`; no capture, market-data query, label generation, or performance scoring is authorized. Overlapping forecast ledgers address R007's arithmetic coverage cap in principle, but do not show the signal can reach 10% coverage or 80% precision. Before any outcome work, separate (1) conditional precision, (2) same-anchor/same-geometry feature ablations, and (3) common-calendar whole-policy P&L into distinct estimands; fully specify the policy baseline and all execution/capital rules; and obtain independent approval of a valid dependence-aware inference and multiplicity procedure. Exact calibration and minimum sample counts, data rights/retention, UTC dates, recorder recovery, multi-symbol capacity, cost calibration, and storage also remain open. No R005/R012 outcomes or performance metrics were generated.
