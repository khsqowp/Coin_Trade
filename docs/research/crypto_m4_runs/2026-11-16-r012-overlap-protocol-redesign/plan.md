# R012 — R005 v1 protocol redesign for overlapping forecasts

Status: `blocked_protocol_draft`; no market outcomes, forecast labels, threshold performance or validation/final aggregates were read. This is a new preregistration proposal, not an amendment to or reuse of R005 results.

## Question and hypothesis

Does the R005 displayed-liquidity-replenishment-after-aggressive-flow mechanism produce selective, target-first long/short forecasts, conditional on the same completed-bar BTC regime, with at least 80% target-first accuracy, at least 10% anchor coverage, and positive net expectancy after measured costs?

The estimand is forecast quality, not simultaneous portfolio performance. Overlapping forecast records are allowed to remove the R007 mechanical coverage ceiling; they are not independent observations and must never be interpreted as concurrent independent trades or multiplied returns.

## Prior trial and non-reuse rule

- R005: 108 proposed cells, zero performance cells and no signal labels.
- R007: exact mathematical cap is about 5% per timeframe under the full-20T suppression, below the frozen 10% coverage gate.
- R008: overlap is only a proposed inference design; it did not freeze a complete protocol.
- R010: two-symbol, one-hour ingestion/replay integrity pilot only; no reconnection was observed and no predictive outcomes were tested.
- R012 changes event overlap, coverage accounting and uncertainty inference before any label is generated. It reuses no R005/R007/R008 outcome because none exists. All R005 feature logic and the 108-cell development grid stay fixed; there is no threshold expansion.

## Frozen proposal

### Universe, opportunity, and timeframe

- At prospective start, snapshot the first 20 instruments ranked by 24-hour quote volume among exchange metadata instruments that are `TRADING`, `PERPETUAL`, USDT-quoted, and COIN-underlying; exclude stock/commodity tokens. Do not replace cohort members; preserve unavailable intervals and reasons.
- Analyze each timeframe independently: `1m, 3m, 5m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 60m, 120m, 240m, 360m, 720m, 1440m`.
- A scheduled opportunity is one frozen-cohort symbol × completed T bar during the fixed final period, whether or not the feed/model can issue a prediction. Neutral regimes, no-event bars, abstentions, and feed-ineligible bars remain in the coverage denominator. Feed-ineligible bars cannot issue a signal and are separately reported by reason. Acceptance coverage = `emitted forecasts / scheduled opportunities`; feed-valid opportunity coverage is diagnostic only. Feed outages cannot inflate acceptance coverage.
- During development, an opportunity key is symbol × timeframe × completed T bar × frozen grid cell; at most one candidate signal per key, using the earliest qualifying burst. Retries are idempotent on that key and never create extra forecasts. Remove R005's cross-timeframe and full-20T event suppression. Signals from different timeframes, cells, or consecutive bars may overlap. These are forecast records only; no portfolio return is formed by summing overlapping outcomes.
- During validation/final, only the one cell and confidence threshold frozen for that timeframe may issue a prediction, at most once per symbol × T bar. The final opportunity key excludes unselected cells. Report long, short, symbol, timeframe, and UTC-day coverage. Coverage must be at least 10% per tested timeframe; pooling frames is prohibited. Actual coverage is an outcome gate, not an assumption.

### Signal and execution outcome

- Preserve R005's completed-bar BTC regime rule, 10-second aggression buckets, displayed-depth replenishment feature, and frozen grid: aggression trailing-30-day percentiles `{80,90,95}` × replenishment minima `{0.5,1.0,1.5}` = 9 cells per timeframe, 108 total. Quantile features use strictly prior observations and availability timestamps. No parameter refinement is permitted in this version.
- Preserve R005 entry: first valid executable ask after a long signal or bid after a short signal is actually available. Persist signal-available time, anchor close, first executable entry timestamp/price, and the full immutable forecast record.
- Preserve R005 geometry: stop one exchange tick beyond the adverse trade extreme in the qualifying burst; reject geometry when R is zero, exceeds 2× ATR14 of the last completed T bar, or is below twice modeled round-trip cost; target is 1R. Primary deadline is 20T; 10T is a secondary checkpoint on the same forecast, never a second sample.
- Fully observed outcome classes are `TARGET_FIRST`, `STOP_FIRST`, `TIMEOUT_NO_TARGET`, and `INVALIDATED_BEFORE_ENTRY`. Timeout is a fully observed non-win through 20T. Invalidation is a fully observed non-win because a barrier was touched before executable entry. Any post-signal path gap or corrupted label window is `UNRESOLVED_DATA`, not a market win or loss.
- Primary accuracy = `TARGET_FIRST / (TARGET_FIRST + STOP_FIRST + TIMEOUT_NO_TARGET + INVALIDATED_BEFORE_ENTRY)`. Its denominator is resolved emitted forecasts; the separate coverage denominator is all scheduled opportunities. Report label-resolution rate (`resolved emitted / all emitted`) and unresolved count/reasons. Any unresolved forecast in the sealed period makes that timeframe `INCONCLUSIVE`; publish `TARGET_FIRST / all emitted` as an all-unresolved-treated-as-loss sensitivity, not as primary accuracy or a substitute pass. This keeps feed failure distinct from forecast correctness without silently dropping missing paths.
- `Emitted` is a Boolean per opportunity key, never a raw retry/message count. A stable forecast ID is derived from run, selected model/cell version, symbol, timeframe and anchor timestamp; duplicate IDs are idempotently rejected. Final issue windows are fixed UTC intervals `[S_T,E_T)`. Stop accepting anchors at `E_T`; continue recording until every issued forecast reaches its own entry-time+20T deadline and lineage/hash checks finish. Only then seal the immutable final ledger. Any path unresolved at that point makes the timeframe inconclusive.

### Costs, comparison, and dependence

- Retain R005 taker entry/exit fee assumption of 0.05% per side until replaced by a dated, pre-outcome user-specific schedule; include observed spread and depth-walk slippage for 1,000 USDT notional, 2× fee/slippage stress, and actual funding if a forecast crosses a funding timestamp. If costs cannot be measured from the permitted data, expectancy is unscorable and no accuracy result qualifies as a useful trading signal.
- **Baseline blocker discovered before outcomes:** R005's proposed macro-only direction comparator uses the candidate's identical anchors and geometry. Since the candidate emits only when its direction equals the confirmed BTC regime, that comparator has the same direction on every candidate anchor and is tautological; it cannot be beaten. Do not use it as an acceptance comparator or generate labels under that comparison.
- **Baseline estimands are unresolved:** remove the tautological same-anchor macro-only comparator. A policy-level comparison may score each fully specified policy on its own emitted anchors, but it measures whole-policy performance, not isolated signal quality. Validation-frozen coverage thresholds do not guarantee equal final coverage or equal anchor/market-regime composition; report each policy's realized final coverage and never call it test-set coverage-matched. Freeze all baseline eligibility, direction, score, tie, randomization seed/scope, and execution rules before labels. The candidate-vs-baseline comparison must use an explicitly defined common-calendar policy P&L series, including zero-return/no-signal intervals and fixed capital/overlap/exposure rules, if the claim is policy performance.
- **Separate policy performance from signal-selection ablation:** policy-level net-return comparison retains each policy's own predeclared stop/target geometry and must not be interpreted as signal-only superiority. A selection-effect ablation must use the same anchors, entry, stop, target, deadline, fill/cost rules and change only the named feature (for example replenishment on/off); it answers a narrower component question and is not a causal proof. Do not merge these estimands into one composite claim.
- **Inference is not yet specified well enough to run:** synchronous calendar blocks preserve shared time shocks but do not create paired observations for policies with different anchors. Exact estimands, test statistics, studentization/variance estimator, null-centering, missing-period treatment, block construction for overlapping labels and valid one-sided p-value procedure remain open. Consequently, the draft `p_T=max(...)` composite and current Holm acceptance language are withdrawn. No bootstrap confidence bound or p-value may be reported as confirmatory until a statistician-reviewed algorithm and simulation/coverage checks are frozen. Holm may be applied only to valid predeclared p-values and the complete claim family, including any development selection that informs claims.
- Any future bootstrap must resample synchronized UTC calendar blocks across all symbols and timeframe ledgers, preserve the full common evaluation calendar, and recompute policy-level outcomes with no-signal periods included. Sensitivity block lengths, seed, replicate count, edge handling and unresolved-feed handling must be frozen. These requirements alone do not establish valid inference or adequate power. Do not treat symbols, overlapping rows, the 10T checkpoint, or different timeframes as independent samples.

### Chronology and decision gates

- Warm-up 30 days; training/development 60 days; calibration 30 days; validation/selection 30 days; then one sealed prospective period. All exact UTC start/end instants, cohort metadata, code/config hashes, cost inputs, data schema and retention policy must be frozen before label aggregation.
- Validation selects at most one confidence threshold per timeframe, by greatest coverage among thresholds meeting point accuracy ≥80%, coverage ≥10%, positive base- and stress-cost expectancy, and existing symbol/day count requirements. Ties: higher dependence-aware lower confidence bound, then lower turnover, then ascending threshold. Final is one-shot and cannot be inspected early or used for retuning.
- Statistical comparator gate is closed pending resolution of separate estimands for conditional forecast precision, matched-geometry selection ablation and common-calendar policy performance. Do not subtract per-anchor precision across policies with different anchors. Do not use the withdrawn macro-only same-anchor comparator or the withdrawn `p_T=max(...)` composite. Baseline definitions, validation selection, complete multiplicity family, test statistics and valid inference procedure must be externally reviewed and frozen before outcomes.
- **No confirmatory pass rule is active.** The numeric gates above are provisional design constraints only; the precision confidence-bound method and policy-performance inference are not yet validly specified. The withdrawn Holm-adjusted composite is not a pass criterion. No result may be called a pass until the complete estimand, valid inference, multiplicity family and acceptance rule have passed independent review and are frozen before outcomes.
- Minimum final calendar span is fixed by timeframe before capture: `max(365 days, 200 × 20T, 30 × L_max)`, where `L_max=max(60T, 30 days)`. For 24/7 crypto, minimum spans are 900 days for 1m–4h, 1,000 days for 6h, 2,000 days for 12h, and 4,000 days (about 11 years) for 1d. Upper bounds on non-overlapping 20T windows in a 365-day span are: 1m 26,280; 3m 8,760; 5m 5,256; 10m 2,628; 15m 1,752; 30m 876; 1h 438; 2h 219; 4h 109.5; 6h 73; 12h 36.5; 1d 18.25. These are arithmetic time-window counts, not empirical independence or power guarantees. If rights or infrastructure cannot support a timeframe's fixed horizon, it remains `INCONCLUSIVE`; do not shorten it after outcomes.
- This draft intentionally preserves the 10% coverage and 80% accuracy targets and tightens the confidence requirement. It does not relax a frozen requirement. Because no valid L2/trade history, approved rights decision, complete pipeline, cost calibration, or minimum calibration counts currently exist, it does not authorize capture or label generation.
