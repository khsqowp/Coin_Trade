## OOS account boundary

The IS and OOS segments are separate accounts, each initialized to NAV 1.0 in cash. A target/order generated before the first OOS observation is not carried into the OOS account. The first OOS trade must be generated by a signal whose close date is on or after the first OOS market observation, then executed on its next observed market date. No IS holdings, pending orders, or equity are transferred.

This resolves the earlier phrase “may use cached ... month-end targets strictly before 2023,” which did not specify whether an IS-generated pending order could execute in the separately initialized OOS account. The chosen policy favors a clean segment reset and makes every OOS order attributable to an OOS signal. The alternative seeded path is retained as a boundary sensitivity only and is not a second independent trial or a canonical result.

This is a methodological clarification, not a strategy or parameter change. It does not remove survivor-cohort, price-adjustment, or licensing limitations. All results remain exploratory only.
