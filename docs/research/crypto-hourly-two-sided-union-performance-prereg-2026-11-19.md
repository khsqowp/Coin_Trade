# Hourly TA-Lib two-sided union — development performance preregistration

## Status and limits

Status: **DRAFT; LOCK BEFORE OUTCOME ACCESS; development-only, exploratory WFA, not confirmatory OOS.** The two-sided union is a post-hoc candidate formed after 55 individual-pattern and directional-union frequency counts over the full exposed history were viewed. That full-history candidate selection contaminates any historical walk-forward interpretation: fold test windows are not independent candidate-discovery OOS. WFA below measures only a frozen training-time barrier-selection procedure conditional on this already-selected signal family; it cannot validate the candidate or estimate untouched OOS performance. This selection history remains part of the research trial family.

No target/stop outcomes, future price paths, return labels, or performance results for this candidate have been read as of this registration. The current Binance Vision history is exposed development data. Any eventual success claim requires a separately approved, prospectively sealed data source and one final evaluation after this registration.

## Candidate, input identity, and signal

- Candidate: at most one signal per symbol-hour. Long if one or more of the 30 bullish Table 2 pattern functions have positive output and the six consecutive changes `SMA144[t-5:t+1] - SMA144[t-6:t]` are all strictly negative. Short if one or more of the 25 bearish pattern functions have negative output and those six changes are all strictly positive. If neither condition holds, no signal. Same-side patterns are deduplicated; the strict opposite SMA-slope conditions make both directions mutually exclusive. No pattern subset, voting score, parameter filter, or manual override is allowed.
- TA-Lib Python 0.6.5; 40 unique function IDs and the frozen positive/negative output rules in [`hourly feasibility preregistration`](crypto-hourly-talib-pattern-feasibility-prereg-2026-11-19.md).
- Signal timestamp: close of a complete UTC hour, after its last 1-minute constituent bar. Entry: next UTC 1-minute bar open. No same-hour-close fills.
- Volatility unit: Wilder ATR(14) over completed hourly OHLC, available at signal close; compute once and freeze for that signal.
- Input file: `chart_predictor/data/vision_pit_dev_sample.sqlite`; manifest SHA-256 `63dd2082c07279f4e2aa8b36df5b6a8dd17512a097f1f4babb73e4a9ef97d45d`; frozen 86-symbol archive-manifest cohort; interval `[2021-01-01T00:00:00Z, 2026-11-01T00:00:00Z)`. This is not a proven point-in-time listing/universe and may contain survivorship or delisting bias.
- Candidate identity is the exact Boolean union above, the 30/25 label mapping, TA-Lib 0.6.5, 144-hour SMA trend rule, and input manifest digest. This registration is not locked until the evaluator, dependencies, and all specification tests are committed. The frozen commit SHA, Git blob hashes for candidate/scanner/evaluator/tests, dependency lock hash, database manifest digest, command line, Python version, TA-Lib Python/core versions, and random seeds must be copied into a lock record before the evaluator opens any post-signal price. Any code or rule change creates a new exploratory trial and invalidates this registration for the changed version.

## Barrier grid and target-first outcome

Test the full frozen 3×3 grid, without choosing a winner after viewing development results:

- Target distance in completed-signal ATR units: `1.00, 1.50, 2.00`.
- Stop distance in completed-signal ATR units: `0.50, 0.75, 1.00`.
- Run every target×stop pair at both `10h` and `20h`: 18 reported diagnostic cells. SPEC-compliant selection/final-gate cells must also satisfy target:stop ratio `target_multiple / stop_multiple` in `[2.0, 4.0]`; 6 barrier pairs (12 horizon cells) are eligible and 3 pairs (6 horizon cells) outside that ratio are robustness diagnostics only and can never be selected or pass the user-facing strategy gate.
- Long target/stop: `entry + target×ATR`, `entry − stop×ATR`. Short target/stop: `entry − target×ATR`, `entry + stop×ATR`.
- Scan 1-minute OHLC beginning with the entry bar through the deadline. First barrier wins. If both barriers lie within the same minute candle, stop is first. If the minute opens beyond the stop, fill at that worse open; a favorable gap through target fills at the target level. No intrabar path is invented.
- For each issued signal, the M2 denominator consists of every signal whose entry and complete future path are evaluable. Its numerator is the number for which target is first reached by the inclusive deadline. `TIMEOUT` is an evaluable M2 miss. `UNRESOLVED_DATA` (missing next-minute entry, missing/incomplete path, or end-of-data censor) is never imputed as success or failure and is reported outside the M2 denominator, consistent with `docs/chart_predictor/SPEC.md`. If unresolved signals exceed 1.00% of all issued signals for a claim/fold, that claim/fold is `INCONCLUSIVE`; no M2 pass/fail decision is allowed. Report all-issued count, resolved M2 denominator, unresolved count/rate, timeout count, and target-first count together. The 1.00% threshold is a fixed data-quality gate, not an outcome-based filter.
- Entry minute is included in scanning. Deadline is the timestamp `signal_close + H hours` and is inclusive: only minute bars whose close timestamp is at or before that deadline may trigger a barrier. The final included bar is the bar ending at the deadline. If both barriers are inside one bar, stop wins. Stop gap fills at the worse open; favorable target gaps fill at the target limit. Fees and slippage apply after gross fill price is determined.
- All issued signals count in M2, including overlapping prediction windows. Separately compute an outcome-blind non-overlap cohort per symbol and horizon by ascending signal-close timestamp: retain the first signal and next retain a signal iff `signal_close >= prior_retained_signal_close + horizon`; exact expiry is eligible. Report its issued count and symbol count; this cohort qualifies sample gates only and never replaces the M2 denominator. N is not treated as independent observations.

## Costs, baselines, and execution separation

- Fixed fee/slippage base assumption: 5 bp taker fee plus 5 bp adverse slippage on entry, and the same on exit (20 bp round trip). Also report 1.5× and 2× adverse slippage while holding fees fixed; these are stress cases, not claims about the user's actual fee tier. Fee is charged on absolute filled notional at each entry/exit; slippage is applied adversely to each fill price, including a stop-gap fill. No leverage or liquidation mechanics are simulated.
- The available local database has candle/manifest/rejected-archive tables only; no funding-rate table or local funding files were found. M2 itself is price-barrier based and can be measured without funding. **Funding-excluded expectancy is not sufficient for a positive-net-expectancy pass.** If a cell remains viable, obtain a separately rights-cleared historical funding input and preregister/join it before calculating the final net expectancy. Do not silently set funding to zero.
- Baseline 1: same candidate signal timestamps and the selected/fixed barrier distances, with direction set by the sign of the immediately preceding completed hourly return. If that return is exactly zero, record `NO_DIRECTION`; for matched target-first comparison it is a baseline miss on that candidate anchor, retained in the paired denominator. Report its frequency separately.
- Baseline 2: same candidate signal timestamps, barrier distances, entry availability, horizon, and path rules; direction is determined by UTF-8 bytes of `hourly-union-v1|<symbol>|<signal_close_ms_decimal>`, with decimal integer UTC milliseconds and no newline. SHA-256 first digest byte even means long, odd means short. Zero-return momentum baseline anchors are marked `NO_DIRECTION` but retained in paired denominator accounting; no row is silently discarded.
- Flat/no-trade is a zero-net-return policy comparator only; M2 is undefined for it.
- Prediction scoring and portfolio execution are separate. M2 retains every issued signal. For realized trade-return/MDD analysis, allow at most one open position per symbol; ignore execution of new same-symbol signals until the prior trade exits, but retain those signals in M2. Report ignored execution signals. No leverage or live orders are simulated.

## Walk-forward, multiplicity, and robustness

- Only exploratory development walk-forward, conditional on the post-hoc candidate: UTC expanding training window of at least 24 months, followed by six-month test windows, stepping six months. First train `[2021-01-01, 2023-01-01)`, first test `[2023-01-01, 2023-07-01)`, continuing through the full six-month test ending `[2026-01-01, 2026-07-01)`. Do not use a partial final fold. For horizon `H`, eligible train anchors satisfy `train_start <= t` and `t+H <= B-20h`; eligible test anchors satisfy `B+20h <= t` and `t+H <= test_end`. Signals inside the 20-hour boundary purge or outside these label-contained anchor ranges are not assigned to that fold and are reported as boundary exclusions. For each fold/horizon, the scheduled coverage denominator is frozen cohort size × the count of hourly timestamps in that same eligible-anchor interval, whether data/feature/signal is available or not; the numerator is issued signals in it. This interval-specific WFA coverage is not the final sealed-period coverage. Count all scheduled slots, data-unavailable slots, feature-ineligible slots, signals, and boundary exclusions separately. No future path may be assigned to more than one fold. Data-end-censored signals outside the label-contained range are reported separately, not reclassified as a fold signal.
- In each training window, consider only SPEC-compliant target/stop pairs whose ratio `target_multiple / stop_multiple` is `[2.0,4.0]`. Apply gates in this order: unresolved rate ≤1.00%; resolved-signal M2 point estimate ≥80%; interval-specific scheduled-slot coverage ≥10%; outcome-blind non-overlap N≥200 across ≥20 symbols; then positive base-cost expectancy excluding funding. A pair failing any earlier gate is not ranked. Select the remaining pair with the highest training net R; deterministic ties choose lower stop multiple, then lower target multiple. If no pair qualifies, the fold abstains. Since funding is absent, this is explicitly a provisional training selector, not fully costed optimization. Test-window outcomes never select that fold's parameters.
- Keep inference targets separate: (a) the 18 fixed barrier×horizon cells and their paired fixed baselines; (b) the entire training-only selection algorithm's WFA test-fold sequence. Do not use an interval for (a) as evidence for (b). Preserve the 110 individual-pattern×deadline, four directional-union×deadline, two two-sided-union×deadline frequency rows, the 18 performance cells, all failed cells, and full post-hoc selection history. Any family-wise p/interval for the historical candidate is exploratory because candidate discovery used the full exposed period.
- For (a), use 10,000 paired circular moving-block bootstrap replicates, block length four consecutive UTC weeks, resampling each sampled week with all symbols and outcomes together. Compute studentized differences in resolved M2 versus each fixed baseline and a single max-|t| statistic over 18 cells × both baselines; use the 95th percentile of that maximum to form simultaneous two-sided 95% intervals. Fixed RNG seed: `20261119`. Do not split symbols independently or discard empty sampled weeks. Also report 2-week and 8-week block-length sensitivity as descriptive, not alternate pass gates. For (b), pool each WFA test signal once with the barrier pair selected by its training fold; compare target-first indicators to both baselines on those same anchors and barrier distances. Use 10,000 paired four-week circular moving-block draws over UTC test weeks, retaining all symbols, and one max-|t| adjustment over the two baseline differences; report the 95% simultaneous interval for the selected procedure. This remains descriptive development WFA, not sealed OOS. Dependence unit is calendar week, retaining all symbols together.
- Run exactly 1,000 permutations of the order of weekly realized R blocks (not bootstrap resampling), with every symbol's weekly R carried together; use seed `20261120`. Assign each closed trade's net R to its UTC exit week, sum within each week, and form the additive equity series from a fixed initial 100R; weekly order is permuted while within-week aggregation is held fixed. Report observed and permutation median/95th-percentile peak-to-trough drawdown in R and percent of 100R. This is order-sensitivity analysis only, not a probability forecast, not a risk-of-ruin estimate, and not concurrent portfolio MDD; it omits intra-week mark-to-market and cross-symbol concurrent-cap effects. No position-size or leverage recommendation is made without a user-approved concurrent-risk cap.
- Parameter sensitivity is the full 3×3 target/stop grid. Report all 18 cells and all eight immediate neighbors of center `(target=1.50, stop=0.75)` on identical test folds, separately at base/1.5×/2× slippage. Among those eight neighbors, exactly five satisfy the fixed SPEC target:stop interval `[2.0,4.0]`; show the other three as ineligible diagnostics. A descriptive legal-grid plateau requires all five eligible neighbors to meet M2≥80%, unresolved rate≤1%, and positive base-cost expectancy on the same fold and horizon cohort; evaluate the 10h and 20h horizons separately. Funding remains absent, so a plateau can never establish fully costed success. Plateau is a robustness diagnostic, not an additional tuning or winner-selection rule.

## Reporting and stop rules

For each of the 18 cells and WFA folds report issued count, resolved M2 numerator/denominator and point estimate, unresolved rate and `INCONCLUSIVE` status, dependency-aware simultaneous interval, direction-endpoint accuracy separately, coverage, non-overlap N and symbol dispersion, target/stop/timeout/unresolved counts, per-symbol and per-fold metrics, paired baseline differences, gross and base/1.5×/2× fee-slippage R, funding status, and the 1,000-permutation order-sensitivity MDD distribution.

The fixed final success gate remains the SPEC: in the prospectively sealed evaluation, resolved-signal M2 point estimate ≥80% with unresolved rate ≤1.00% (otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE`); coverage ≥10% of all scheduled cohort symbol×hour slots; outcome-blind non-overlap N≥200 across ≥20 symbols; dependency-adjusted simultaneous lower bound above both signal baselines; positive fully costed expectancy; and target:stop ratio `[2.0,4.0]`. One final sealed evaluation is permitted. A development fold cannot pass this gate. Missing funding data, lack of a point-in-time contract universe, or lack of a prospectively sealed data source leaves the strategy **unverified**, even if M2 is high.

Do not run outcome-path evaluation until this file, the exact evaluator, its lock record and tests are reviewed and committed together as the frozen preregistration. A future source download for funding is permitted only for historical personal non-production backtesting under applicable data terms; raw data must remain out of GitHub.
