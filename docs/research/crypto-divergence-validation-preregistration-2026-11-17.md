# Crypto divergence study: preregistration and mandatory robustness gates

Status: preregistered design and tested validation primitives, including a long/short OHLC path resolver; the market-data divergence backtest has not yet run, so no strategy result is available.

## Decision rule

This is a research-only long/short study on historical crypto perpetual candles. No exchange order/API calls are permitted. A candidate is rejected if it fails any of the three mandatory robustness tests below, or the repository's frozen signal-quality contract. A promising in-sample score is never sufficient for promotion.

The frozen primary signal metric is M2 target-first precision, calculated over every emitted OOS signal including overlaps, with target-first defined as a target touched before stop/invalidation and within the registered horizon. Timeout and same-path target/stop ties are failures. Report deadline direction accuracy separately. In addition, require an outcome-blind non-overlap sample of at least 200 signals and 20 distinct symbols for eligibility. Report coverage, fee/slippage-adjusted expectancy, uncertainty, and same-anchor baselines; do not describe M2 as profitability.

## Hypothesis and signal timing

Hypothesis: a regular bullish divergence (lower confirmed price low and higher oscillator value at the two pivot anchors) during a bullish closed-4h macro regime identifies an intraday pullback resumption; a regular bearish divergence (higher confirmed price high and lower oscillator value) during a bearish closed-4h macro regime identifies a rally resumption.

Primary oscillator is RSI(14); MACD histogram (12,26,9) is a separately reported, pre-registered replication family, not a substitute selected after seeing results. The 4h regime is close above EMA(50) and EMA(50)>EMA(200) for longs, mirrored for shorts. A pivot with lag L is usable only at its confirmation candle; the oscillator is sampled at the pivot anchor, not the confirmation bar. Signal features use closed candles only. Earliest fill is the next executable bar open. No signal is backdated to its pivot anchor.

## Frozen candidate grid

- Timeframes: 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 60m; outcome horizons: 10 and 20 native bars.
- Pivot confirmation lag L: 2, 3, 4 bars.
- Entry: next-bar open; or stop-entry only after signal-bar high (long) / low (short) breaks, valid for 3 bars, otherwise expire.
- Stop: latest confirmed divergence pivot plus 0 or 0.25 ATR(14) adverse buffer, measured at signal time.
- Target: 1.0R, 1.5R, 2.0R, or 3.0R; time exit at registered horizon. A newly confirmed opposite divergence is reported as a diagnostic exit only and is not tuned into the primary grid.
- Fees: repository contract's round-trip 0.10%; add a separately reported 2x-slippage stress using adverse execution on entry and exit. Use minute-path stop-first resolution where data supports it; unknown path ordering fails conservatively.
- No further indicators, thresholds, filters, or grid expansion after OOS results are viewed.

## Deterministic signal, execution, coverage, and selection rules (frozen before labels)

- A pivot at anchor `a` with lag `L` is confirmed at `a+L` using the centered window `[a-L, a+L]`. A low/high is a pivot only when it is the unique minimum/maximum in that window; any tied extreme is rejected. The anchor must be at least `L` bars after the immediately previous same-side pivot anchor. Compare only with the immediately previous confirmed same-side pivot, even if an opposite-side pivot intervenes. Long divergence requires strictly lower price low and strictly higher oscillator value; short divergence requires strictly higher price high and strictly lower oscillator value. Equality is not a divergence. No minimum amplitude threshold is added. Each confirmed pivot produces at most one comparison per side and timeframe; no duplicate signal is emitted for the same `(symbol, timeframe, oscillator, side, anchor, lag)`.
- Signal is available only at the close of its confirmation candle. ATR(14) for the stop buffer is the causal value at that close. A proposed stop on the wrong side of the signal-close price, nonpositive entry-to-stop risk, or a stop already crossed before entry invalidates the candidate trade; it is not shifted or repaired.
- Next-open entry uses the next native bar open. Stop-entry is active on the next three native bars, inclusive. A long trigger is signal-bar high and a short trigger is signal-bar low. If a bar opens through the trigger, fill at that open (never the better trigger price). If the stop is crossed before the trigger, classify the emitted signal as a resolved no-trade/failure. If no trigger occurs in the three bars, classify as resolved expired/no-trade/failure. Once entered, the target/stop horizon begins on the entry bar and covers exactly the next `H` native bars including that bar. If entry price is already through a stop or target barrier, classify as invalidated-before-entry/no-trade, not a filled trade.
- The closed 4h series is formed from complete UTC-aligned 4h intervals only. Its availability time is interval end; a native signal may use only the latest valid 4h close whose availability time is no later than the signal candle close. EMA(50)/EMA(200) use close-only recursive EMA and require 200 consecutive valid 4h closes for a usable state. A missing or malformed 4h interval breaks availability; no forward-fill across it. The macro filter is unavailable until a fresh run of 200 consecutive valid closes exists.
- Base fee is 0.05% of notional on entry plus 0.05% on exit, charged once each. The 2x-slippage stress assumes adverse 0.025% per side in the base case and adverse 0.05% per side in stress, on top of fees; this is a frozen sensitivity assumption, not observed slippage. Apply costs to executable prices and identically to candidate and matched baselines. Invalid/nonpositive risk distances are unresolved data-quality/invalid-trade counts and never silently clipped.
- Outcome path resolution uses the highest-resolution stored 1m candles. For a 1m candidate, its own bar is the minimum path unit. Stop wins if stop and target are both touched in the same minimum-resolution bar. Stop gaps fill at the adverse observed open; favorable target gaps fill at the target. Missing/duplicate/non-monotonic/malformed required path bars are unresolved. No-fill/expiry and horizon timeout are resolved target-first failures. The M2 primary denominator includes all emitted signals with resolved labels; unresolved labels are reported separately and excluded from that resolved-only fraction. A claim/fold is INCONCLUSIVE unless at least 95% of emitted signals have resolved paths; also publish the conservative sensitivity that treats every unresolved signal as a failure.
- The frozen family contains exactly 20 claims: 2 oscillator families × 5 native timeframes × 2 horizons. Each claim is evaluated separately; horizons/timeframes are never pooled to satisfy sample size. Within each claim and training fold, choose only among the 48 cells formed by lag (3), entry (2), stop buffer (2), target (4). A candidate is selectable only if its training M2 is at least 80%, it has at least 30 resolved signals, positive net expectancy after base costs, passes the plateau rule, and meets 95% path coverage. Ties are resolved in this order: higher M2, higher net expectancy, then lexicographic `(lag ascending, next-open before stop-entry, stop-buffer ascending, target-R ascending)`. If no cell qualifies, that fold has no selected candidate.
- For inference against the two same-anchor baselines, every emitted candidate anchor is retained. Baseline directions are previous-bar close-to-close momentum (exact zero falls back to the same frozen SHA-256 fair-direction rule) and an independently keyed SHA-256 fair direction. Both baseline policies enter at the common next-native-bar open, using the candidate's ex-ante stop distance, target-R, registered horizon, fees, and 1m path rules; they do not inherit candidate direction or delayed stop-entry timing. This is a same-anchor policy comparison, not an entry-fill-matched execution comparison. A no-fill candidate signal remains a resolved M2 failure; the baseline still has its next-open hypothetical label. The new 20-claim/40-contrast family requires its own synthetic calibration before any baseline-superiority promotion; earlier 24-claim/48-contrast R019 artifacts are not treated as an exact-family calibration.
- At every WFA boundary purge all training outcomes whose maximum event interval can reach the test boundary: 3 native bars of stop-entry activation plus 20 native bars of outcome, i.e. 23 native bars. This 23-bar rule supersedes the shorter 20-bar wording below. OOS signals without the complete registered entry/outcome interval before the fold's exclusive test end are censored and reported, not scored as wins or losses. Select parameters on the 504-day training interval only, then score that frozen choice on the next 252-day test interval. Report each claim and each fold, including empty or no-selection folds.
- Apply the required 1,000 trade-order permutations to each fixed claim's pooled OOS realized net-R sequence from filled trades only, using a deterministic, recorded claim-specific seed and 1% equity risk per trade. No-fill/expired/invalidated signals have zero realized portfolio P&L and are excluded from the trade sequence, while remaining M2 failures; report emitted signals and filled trades separately. Also report each fold's sample size and MDD; do not imply the pooled permutation simulates concurrent positions or signal dependence.
- Track two distinct coverage gates: (a) ≥95% of emitted signals must have resolved outcome paths, otherwise the claim/fold is INCONCLUSIVE; (b) per-timeframe issue coverage must meet SPEC's ≥10% of all scheduled frozen-universe symbol × completed-bar opportunities. The 95% path-resolution ratio cannot substitute for issue coverage. Count every eligible symbol-bar opportunity, including no-signal and macro-unavailable opportunities, in the latter denominator.

## Mandatory tests

1. **Trade-order Monte Carlo:** for each frozen OOS candidate, reorder its complete realized net-R outcome multiset 1,000 times with a deterministic recorded seed. At a fixed 1% equity risk per trade, report historical chronological MDD, permutation MDD median/95th percentile/maximum, and 95th-percentile MDD. This is a sequence-risk diagnostic, not a market-path forecast; it does not model changing trade distributions, signal dependence, or simultaneous portfolio exposure. Do not claim live position sizing from it.
2. **Walk-forward:** five chronological windows, each trained on 504 calendar days and tested on the immediately following 252 calendar days; advance by 252 calendar days. Apply the same date boundaries to every intraday timeframe, so a 504/252 split does not collapse into a few hours on 1m data. Select only from the frozen grid using training data, then freeze parameters throughout that test window. Purge all overlapping train events using the frozen maximum 23-native-bar event interval. Report every window, including empty/failed windows, pooled OOS outcomes, and train-to-test degradation. No test-window retuning. At least 1,764 days of usable history plus 4h-indicator warm-up are required; if the dataset cannot supply this, report `INCONCLUSIVE` rather than shortening the windows.
3. **Parameter sensitivity:** on each training window, evaluate all adjacent cells in the frozen grid. Define a passing cell before evaluation as train M2 >=80%, at least 30 resolved training signals, and positive net expectancy after costs. A selected candidate is plateau-robust only if at least 80% of its one-step neighbors pass and the absolute difference between selected-cell M2 and the adjacent-neighbor median is ≤10 percentage points. Compute that median across every expected neighbor; missing, unresolved, or under-sampled neighbors contribute M2=0 as well as failing the pass rule. Report neighbor count and pass fraction; a cell with fewer than 4 neighbors is non-evaluable, not robust. No isolated peak passes.

## Causal and data checks

Before backtesting, unit-test pivot-anchor oscillator sampling, confirmation delay, next-bar entry, closed-4h alignment, stop/target tie handling, timeout, short symmetry, and parameter bounds. The shared OHLC resolver requires observed integer timestamps plus the expected path start and full horizon length; missing leading, interior, trailing, or malformed required bars are unresolved. It applies adverse stop-gap fills and resolves same-bar barrier ties as stop-first. Plateau evaluation requires the complete frozen expected parameter grid; omitted neighbors count as failures. Run prefix-invariance and deliberate future-peeking injection checks. Audit gaps, duplicate candles, symbol listing periods, and delisted/contract universe availability before selecting the data window. Freeze an immutable data fingerprint and trial manifest. Split chronologically; never use final sealed data to choose the grid or filters.

## Promotion gate

`REJECT` if any required Monte Carlo, walk-forward, sensitivity, causal, sample, or frozen SPEC gate fails. `INCONCLUSIVE` if required OOS sample, data provenance, or exact 20-claim/40-contrast baseline calibration is insufficient. The existing R019 calibration covers a different 24-claim/48-contrast family and is not silently substituted. Only a candidate passing all gates can be labeled `FORWARD-TEST CANDIDATE`; this study alone never authorizes live trading. Results must include all attempted cells and negative outcomes.
