The 1m momentum baseline uses the last completed 1m close-to-close return; flat/zero or missing returns abstain and lower `Baseline coverage`. Random direction is deterministic SHA-256 keyed by `round5-mtf-random-v1`, symbol, entry timestamp, and timeframe. Precision is target-first among scored directional baseline forecasts. These are development diagnostics, not a sealed test.

## Decision

Unfiltered q≥2 forecasts did not approach the 80% target-first objective; the best unleveraged 20-bar cell was 240m long at 35.20% (201/571 targets; mean net R +0.134), selected post-hoc among 36 cells. The vote-count slices below are a separate post-hoc development diagnostic. Neither result is independent validation or suitable evidence for live/manual deployment.

The run emitted 244 MTF vote-threshold rows. Eighteen rows show ≥80% precision, but every one has N=1 from one symbol; the largest such slice is only 0.194% of its q≥2 cohort. No row meets the combined development screen of ≥80% precision, ≥10% conditional cohort coverage, N≥200, and ≥20 symbols. Among 90 rows with N≥200, 25 beat both matched directional baselines on precision and 21 beat both on mean net R; these are correlated post-hoc comparisons, not multiplicity-adjusted evidence.

The strongest adequately sized descriptive cell is 240m long/20 bars/q≥2: MTF 35.20% target-first and +0.134 mean net R (N=571, 50 symbols), versus matched 1m momentum 33.58%/+0.114 and fixed-seed random 29.42%/+0.004 on the exact same timestamps and matched risk. At 120m long/20 bars/q≥3, precision is 34.96% (N=226, 21.08% of q≥2 anchors) versus 32.55% momentum and 32.74% random, while mean net R is +0.018 versus −0.016 and +0.022. These small, selected differences do not establish a stable edge; q≥2 coverage is not coverage over all eligible decision times.

## Missing validation gates

- This development interval was previously explored and is not sealed or out-of-sample.
- Universe is the current collection list, not point-in-time membership; survivorship and listing bias remain.
- Confidence-slice coverage is conditional on the q≥2 consensus cohort, not all eligible decision times; event/symbol/day clustered confidence bounds, an always-flat predictive baseline, and slippage/depth are not computed.
- Per-timeframe outcomes share consensus events and symbols and are not independent samples.
- Approximate 4x/5x liquidation uses a fixed maintenance buffer, not Binance account-specific liquidation mechanics.
- Vote-count slices are post-hoc development exploration, not calibrated probabilities or a preregistered threshold.
- Confidence-slice coverage is conditional on the q≥2 consensus cohort within each cell; coverage over all eligible decision times was not computed, so the registered 10% coverage threshold is not tested.
- The directional baselines above are matched development diagnostics, not preregistered or held-out baselines; an always-flat baseline, event/symbol/day clustered bounds, and multiplicity adjustment were not computed.
- The fixed-seed random comparator is one reproducible direction realization, not a distribution over repeated randomized baselines; its comparison uncertainty is not estimated.
- The 80% criterion is plainly missed on descriptive precision, but this is not a complete statistical verdict because the development data were previously viewed and the cohort is not point-in-time.
