# Strategy research round 4 — 2026-11-15

Status: exploratory development only. No strategy is validated, promoted, or authorized for live trading. All equity prices were cut at 2024-11-12 exclusive. Crypto used the read-only `vision_dev.sqlite` cache and a 2026-10-09 exclusive cutoff; the cache itself ends 2026-06-30.

## Fixed hypotheses

1. KR and US ETF relative/absolute momentum: monthly rebalance, rank the prior 126 sessions, hold up to three ETFs with positive 126-session return, equal weight, otherwise cash. Orders are modeled at the next session open. Round-trip costs were modeled as 0.23% base / 0.33% stress for KR ETFs and 0.36% base / 1.22% stress for US ETFs. These are assumed costs, not measured fills.
2. Crypto RSI(2) mean reversion: long below RSI 10, short above RSI 90; next-bar open entry; ATR(14) one-ATR target and stop; 10/20-bar expiry; stop-first same-bar tie; missing-minute paths excluded; mutually non-overlapping events across both sides per symbol. Round-trip costs 0.10% / 0.30%.
3. Crypto Bollinger re-entry: a close outside BB(20,2), then first close back inside; next bar open; one simple-moving-average ATR(14) target/stop; 10/20-bar expiry; stop-first ambiguous OHLC touch; no overlapping trades across sides; incomplete signal windows and missing-minute paths excluded. Round-trip costs 0.10% / 0.30%.

## Data and execution limitations

- The KR/US cache is a present-day cohort, not a point-in-time universe. Delisted/merged constituents and survivorship bias remain unresolved. Price adjustment and dividend provenance were not established in this round.
- Equity cost levels are assumptions; ETF distributions, taxes, market impact, and fill modeling are incomplete. Independent KR/US audits reproduced a preceding version and identified cost-timing and benchmark-cost asymmetry. A subsequent self-review found that its fixed-weight return calculation implicitly rebalanced daily despite monthly signals. That version was discarded. The final runner now reads OHLC only before the exclusive cutoff, starts with cash immediately before the first 2011 session, tracks shares and cash through monthly-open rebalances, lets holdings drift, and charges rebalance plus terminal liquidation costs. Benchmarks include matching entry and exit costs. Separate standard-library KR and US replays reproduced the final CSV metrics. The remaining ETF vintage/survivorship and adjusted-price uncertainties prevent a robust claim.
- Crypto results are unlevered price-path outcomes. They are not actual futures ROI and omit funding, spread, order-book slippage, exchange liquidation, and latency. Stop-distance labels must not be interpreted as a safe leverage level.
- RSI is reproducible with `scripts/research/long_history_rsi2_multitf_probe.py --signal-family rsi2`; its 144 cell-level results are in `docs/research/rsi2_multitf_20261115.csv`. The same runner with `--signal-family bb_reentry` produced `docs/research/bb_reentry_multitf_20261115.csv`. Both are exploratory and not production execution models. Outcome rates are not calibrated probabilities and do not pass the chart-predictor M4 gate.
- Equity ETF screening is reproducible with `scripts/research/equity_etf_rotation_probe.py`; summary rows are in `docs/research/equity_etf_rotation_20261115.csv`. The buy-and-hold benchmarks now include matching entry/exit costs.
- Bollinger run used a read-only SQLite query with `open_ms < 2026-10-09T00:00:00Z`; the detailed agent script/JSON remain in `/private/tmp/bb_atr_dev_probe.py` and `/private/tmp/bb_atr_dev_results.json` and are not repository artifacts. The summary metrics above are preserved here, but that probe is not yet integrated into the repository.
- The first KR agent could not access the checkout, and the first RSI agent inspected only the short-history collector DB. Later independent KR and US audits reproduced the final ETF metrics with separate standard-library calculations. An independent Bollinger code audit confirmed that its original run covered only 1m signals. ## Round 8 — fixed 20-bar support/resistance breakout-retest

Freeze the catalog's S/R retest concept into a deterministic rule. Resistance/support are the highest high/lowest low of the prior 20 completed bars. A close crossing the level starts a pending breakout. Within the next 10 bars, cancel if a long closes back at/below resistance (or a short at/above support), or if price pierces more than 0.5 signal-time ATR beyond the level. A valid retest must touch within 0.25 ATR of the level and close back beyond it in the breakout direction with a matching candle body. Enter next bar open; stop at the confirmation candle's low/high; target 2R; expire after 10/20 bars; resolve same-minute barrier ties stop-first. Non-overlap is enforced per symbol/timeframe through the full horizon. The matched control takes the opposite side at the same anchor with the same risk distance. Costs are 0.10% base / 0.30% stress round trip; raw 1m OHLC resolves outcomes. Use the same 2026-10-09 exclusive cutoff and only BNB/BTC/DOGE/ETH, the four symbols with no known internal minute gaps. No parameter sweep.

Highest target-first was DOGE 2h/20 bars, n=374: 34.76% (moving UTC-calendar-block 95% interval 29.49–39.65%), versus 31.82% for the matched reverse-side control (27.39–36.48%). Block length was the ceiling of each tested holding horizon in calendar days (1–20 days); 1,000 bootstrap draws were used. These per-cell intervals are not corrected for selecting the best out of 96 cells.
