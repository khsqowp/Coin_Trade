# Addendum A2: frozen cross-market execution, holding cap, and data gate
Registered 2026-11-08 KST before v3 strategy calculations. Supersedes conflicting implementation notes in PREREG.md; no strategy outcomes have been calculated.

## Common strategy mechanics
All three strategy implementations use max 20 concurrent positions, regardless of liquidity-universe N=50/100/200; uninvested capital stays cash. Liquidity Top-N only defines which names may be selected. RSI2 remains daily, residual momentum monthly, and gap reversal daily. RSI2 uses close(t)>SMA200 and Wilder RSI2<=10, next available open entry; exit decision close(t)>SMA5 or completed holding age>=5, next available open. Gap is observed at open(t+1), eligible from prior close(t)>SMA200 and liquidity rank at t; enter at close(t+1), exit close(t+2), no same-open fill. Residual uses daily log returns: OLS with intercept and beta over trailing 252 sessions (>=200 paired observations), then sum the latest 126 daily residual log returns (stock log return minus fitted intercept and beta times SPY log return); positive signal, monthly close decision, next-open rebalance, top 20 among eligible liquidity Top-N, equal weight.

## Liquidity ranking check
Cached Yahoo chart fields are open/high/low/close/adjclose/volume. Close is split-adjusted OHLC; adjclose/close contains dividend adjustment. Dollar volume ranking uses raw cache close*volume, avoiding dividend-adjusted price. Spot checks around split dates show no split-sized dollar-volume discontinuity: AAPL 2014-06-06 close $23.0561 × volume 349.94m = $8.07bn; 2014-06-09 close $23.4250 × 301.66m = $7.07bn; AAPL 2020-08-28 = $23.42bn and 2020-08-31 = $29.12bn; NVDA 2024-06-07 = $49.85bn and 2024-06-10 = $38.17bn. This supports split-basis consistency in these observations but is not an exhaustive audit of all vendors or symbols. Ranking remains conditional on the cached Yahoo series; no capitalization data is inferred.

## PIT and quality gate
The point-in-time constituent file contains 1,262 ticker spells and 1,209 unique symbols, while only 717 symbols have a price parquet (492 unique symbols have no file). Historical membership snapshots: 2,720 dates, 1996-01-02 through 2026-08-18, with 503 names in the last snapshot. The companion README says history starts from a 1996-2019 source and has been manually extended periodically from Wikipedia; the observation end is stale by the study end date 2026-11-03. Membership after 2026-08-18 is carried forward and marked stale. Price parquet inventory has 737 files including non-equities/benchmarks; previous v2 data-quality report covers 717 equity series, 4,133,396 rows: 66 rows with missing OHLC, 41 OHLC inconsistencies, 16,791 zero-volume days, 449 adjusted daily returns above 50%, and 205 adjustment-factor jumps. Existing v2 loader excludes 10 known reused-ticker cases and mechanically repairs 3 adjustment histories; these mitigations do not recover missing/delisted history. Prior v2 PIT member-day data availability was 68.23% in 2012, 83.89% in 2019, 87.18% in 2020, 96.75% in 2025, and 98.65% over available 2026 membership days. Increasing coverage reflects newer history and must not be mistaken for complete coverage.

Gate disposition: CONDITIONAL for exploratory computation only, NO-GO for claims of complete US universe, clean PIT validation, or strategy promotion. Rows with invalid/nonpositive OHLC, missing OHLC, or zero volume cannot create new entries/fills. Existing held positions remain marked with the prior valid close across missing rows; no artificial price is created. >50% adjusted-return events are retained and flagged, not clipped; affected-symbol contributions and sensitivity must be disclosed. The stale PIT tail and 492 symbol-level holes are reported. If flags materially affect a result, it cannot pass a strategy gate.

Input identities are frozen in INPUT_HASHES_PRECALC_A2.txt; they include the final preregistration, PIT files, SPY, panel cache, per-parquet SHA256 aggregate, and a data-quality snapshot. No v3 metric was calculated before this addendum.
