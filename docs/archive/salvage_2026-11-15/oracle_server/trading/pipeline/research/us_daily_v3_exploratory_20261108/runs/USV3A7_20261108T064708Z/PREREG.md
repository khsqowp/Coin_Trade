# US daily v3 exploratory preregistration
Registered: 2026-11-08 KST, before calculating v3 strategy results. Backtest only; no orders/config changes. Results are exploratory and contaminated by previous US v1/v2 and market-path observation.

## Scope and data
Use existing Yahoo daily OHLCV and PIT S&P500 membership snapshots in `data/us_daily`; dates 2012-06-01 through 2026-11-03. IS 2012-06-01..2019-12-31; reused OOS 2020-01-01..2026-11-03, not untouched. `hist_components.csv` last snapshot 2026-08-18; the remaining tail is carried forward and is therefore stale membership. History is manually compiled from 1996-2019 source plus periodic Wikipedia changes, not an authoritative comprehensive PIT feed. 737 parquet files do not constitute complete survivorship-free US coverage; delisted/renamed/reused tickers and missing price histories remain. Report available-member coverage and missing symbols.

No point-in-time market capitalization exists. Top 50/100/200 means ranked by contemporaneous trailing 60-session median dollar volume among eligible PIT S&P500 constituents, not company size. Dollar volume = raw close × raw volume. Eligibility: currently in PIT membership, at least 252 valid prior bars, at least 55 valid observations in trailing 60 sessions, and median dollar volume >= USD 5,000,000. This is an operational liquidity filter, not economically equivalent to KRX KRW 1bn.

## Fixed rules; no parameter search
Run each strategy with liquidity-universe N=50,100,200. Use equal weights, long-only, no leverage; if fewer than N eligible/signal names, use available names. Rebalance monthly at last SPY session of month except daily strategies. RSI and residual signal at close t fill at next open. Cost: 10bp of turnover each side, matching US v2 primary assumption; report 5/20/40bp per-side sensitivity. Cash return 0 for consistency with KR v3.

1. RSI(2) trend-filtered mean reversion: Wilder RSI(2) <=10 and close>SMA200 at close; buy next open. At close exit when close>SMA5 or completed holding age >=5 sessions; exit next available open. Rank lowest RSI2; max N slots, equal-weight among open positions. No same-day re-entry after exit.
2. Overnight gap reversal: at open t, eligible only if close(t-1)>SMA200(t-1), trailing liquidity rank <=N, and open(t)/close(t-1)-1 <= -2.5%. Since the gap is known only at the open, enter at close t and exit at close t+1. Rank most-negative gap; take up to N names equally weighted; no overnight hold beyond the close-to-close interval. Missing bars are excluded. This rule is an executable daily-bar approximation; it cannot model intraday path, halts or queue position.
3. SPY residual momentum: monthly at close t estimate beta from OLS of daily simple stock returns on SPY returns over trailing 252 sessions through t, requiring >=200 paired observations and positive benchmark variance. Signal = stock 126-session log return − beta × SPY 126-session log return. Require positive signal, eligible liquidity Top-N and valid beta; select top N, equal-weight; next-open rebalance. This is a cross-sectional stock signal, not a market-neutral portfolio.

## Benchmarks/diagnostics
For each N compare SPY buy-and-hold net of initial 10bp buy cost and monthly rebalanced equal-weight portfolio of the same dynamic liquidity Top-N universe at same 10bp/side. Report IS, reused OOS, full, annual returns, Sharpe (cash=0), max drawdown, turnover, costs, fills, average eligible/top-N counts and regime results (use fixed KR v3 regime definitions where available; disclose unmatched/market-specific periods). Report beta/exposure and concentration for residual momentum if feasible. No strategy is promoted based on reused OOS. No winner is termed successful without a new untouched forward period.

## Overlap disclosure
These are not independent strategy families. RSI2 and gap reversal overlap with short-term reversal / mean-reversion strategy families. Residual momentum overlaps with the Coin_Trade and prior US/KR cross-sectional momentum families. These are defined variants for comparable market/universe tests, not claimed novel independent discoveries. No additional rules are added after outcomes are seen.
