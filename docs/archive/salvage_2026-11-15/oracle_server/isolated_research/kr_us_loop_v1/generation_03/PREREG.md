# Generation 03 — OHLC and volatility-structure candidates (2026-11-06)

## Fixed scope

Five hypotheses, each once in KR and once in US (10 candidate-market attempts). Use only the local market mirror and the shared Gen01 daily next-open simulator, costs, universe eligibility, IS/later partitions and per-run immutable artifacts. Decision states can change only at month-end close; resulting weights are filled at the next available session open. Do not vary any formula or threshold after looking at results. Every setup failure, test failure, start, completion and exception must remain in a JSONL ledger. The historical later windows and candidate families have prior exposure; these are exploratory trials, not clean OOS tests. Prior global trial count is unknown; 66 locally recorded candidate-market executions will exist after this generation (22 original Gen01 executions including the subsequently invalidated run, 22 corrected Gen01, 12 Gen02, 10 Gen03), but this is not a complete historical count and not a valid multiplicity correction.

Current-data limitations remain: KR uses an available-universe proxy rather than KOSPI/KODEX, PIT membership is unresolved, KR split-repair is heuristic, and volume-adjustment emitted divide-by-zero warnings. These candidates intentionally do not use volume. Their performance still depends on OHLC integrity. US price history exists for only a subset of the PIT list. No strategy can pass validation on these known-contaminated windows.

## Frozen candidate rules

All features use data known through month-end `t`; no centered filters, forward shifts, future values, or forward-filled missing OHLC. Long only, unlevered, top K (KR 20 / US 50) where applicable, equal weight; cash for unfilled slots. Ties resolve by ticker. Fees and execution follow Gen01.

1. **FRAMA16_SLOPE5** — For each observed daily close use `N1=(HH8_recent−LL8_recent)/8`, `N2=(HH8_prior−LL8_prior)/8`, `N3=(HH16−LL16)/16`; `D=(ln(N1+N2)−ln(N3))/ln(2)`. If any range is nonpositive/nonfinite, `alpha` and that date's signal/rank are unavailable; the recursive filter carries its prior value. At a month-end when the feature/rank is unavailable, the position is exited at the next executable rebalance rather than carried with an invented rank. `alpha=clip(exp(−4.6*(D−1)),0.01,1)`; recursively `F_t=alpha*C_t+(1−alpha)*F_(t−1)`, reinitialize to close after a missing-price gap. At month-end enter/hold only if `C>F` and `F>F_(t−5)` and feature is available; exit if either fails or the feature is unavailable. Rank by `(F_t−F_(t−5))/63-session close-return standard deviation`; require 200 observed bars after restart.
2. **VORTEX14_SMA100** — `VM+=abs(H_t−L_(t−1))`, `VM−=abs(L_t−H_(t−1))`, `TR=max(H−L,abs(H−C_(t−1)),abs(L−C_(t−1)))`; `VI+=sum(VM+,14)/sum(TR,14)`, `VI−=sum(VM−,14)/sum(TR,14)`. At month-end enter/hold when `VI+>VI−` and `C>SMA100`, otherwise exit. Rank by `VI+−VI−`. Require all 14 consecutive valid OHLC observations plus 100 observed closes; a gap restarts the relevant rolling windows.
3. **ICHIMOKU_CURRENT_CLOUD** — Use only unshifted, currently known values: `Tenkan=(HH9+LL9)/2`, `Kijun=(HH26+LL26)/2`, `SpanA=(Tenkan+Kijun)/2`, `SpanB=(HH52+LL52)/2`. At month-end enter/hold if `C>max(SpanA,SpanB)` and `Tenkan>Kijun`; otherwise exit. Rank by `(C−max(SpanA,SpanB))/C`. Do not use chart-displaced leading spans or any future-aligned values. Require 52 consecutive valid OHLC observations.
4. **DCTC_GAP_INTRADAY63** — Per session `g=ln(O/C_prev)`, `b=ln(C/O)`. For the latest 63 consecutive valid sessions calculate `Tg=mean(g)/(sample_sd(g)/sqrt(63))` and equivalent `Tb`. Invalid/zero standard deviation or any invalid OHLC makes the feature unavailable. Enter at month-end if `Tg>=0.5` and `Tb>=0.5`; once held, retain only while both are `>0`; otherwise exit. Rank by `min(Tg,Tb)`. This decomposes close-to-close trend into overnight and intraday channels; no volume input.
5. **VOL_COMPRESSION_RELEASE55** — `ATR20` is rolling mean of standard true range over 20 consecutive valid OHLC bars; `A=ATR20/C`. At month-end `t`, `base_min` is the minimum of the 42 observations `A[t−62]...A[t−21]`; the compression cutoff is the 20th percentile of the 252 observations `A[t−272]...A[t−21]` (fixed linear quantile). Entry requires `base_min<=cutoff`, `C_t>max(H[t−55]...H[t−1])`, and `A_t>=1.3*base_min`. Once held, retain while `C_t>=min(L[t−20]...L[t−1])`; otherwise exit. Rank by `(C_t/prior55_high−1)/A_t`. Missing/zero/nonfinite values invalidate the signal; no interpolation. This tests compression followed by expansion, not a generic Donchian breakout.

## Reporting and stop rules

- Log a unique run ID, hashes of preregistration/runner/Gen01 dependency, market data coverage, benchmark identity, planned count, every attempt event, full metrics, decision counts, curve hash and file paths.
- Stop a market and log a failure row for every planned candidate if its panel or benchmark is invalid. Do not zero-fill missing benchmark returns or missing market OHLC.
- The current data and partitions cannot establish validity. Even an apparent pass is only a candidate for a later prospective test after PIT/universe, corporate actions, benchmark and historic trial-count reconciliation.
- These five rules are related tests within broader trend/volatility families; they are five new local hypotheses, not five independent discoveries. No DSR/PSR or family-wise significance claim is made.
