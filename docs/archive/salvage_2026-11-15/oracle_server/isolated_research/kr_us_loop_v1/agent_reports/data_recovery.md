# Data recovery scout — KR/US daily research

Checked 2026-11-07. Read-only review of the local mirror and official/vendor documentation; no data was downloaded, altered, or backtested. Oracle/SSH was not accessed.

## Local evidence and exact gaps

- KR mirror: `data_mirror/kr_daily_v2/universe/universe_raw.csv` has 3,730 rows, 3,628 unique codes, and 102 repeated-code rows; only 3,236 matching `.parquet` files exist. Of the 395 unique universe codes without a file, 322 are marked `delisted` and 73 `listed`. The local `kind_stockMkt.html`, `kind_kosdaqMkt.html`, and `kind_delisted.html` files can help reconcile identities/statuses, but are not daily prices or terminal proceeds. `495910.none` is a local no-data marker.
- US mirror: `data_mirror/us_daily/pit/hist_components.csv` and `sp500_ticker_start_end.csv` contain 1,209 unique tickers over 1996-01-02–2026-08-18. The PIT README says this history was assembled from an older book-related list plus Wikipedia and manually researched changes; it explicitly warns some early symbols may be missing and that Yahoo does not retain delisted stocks. Treat it as a provisional PIT history, not an authoritative S&P source.
- Of those 1,209 US names, 737 have local Parquet, 384 have Yahoo HTTP-404/other error sidecars, and 110 have neither a Parquet file nor a missing-sidecar record. The local `SPY.parquet` exists (8,498 rows, 1993-01-29–2026-11-02), but its metadata identifies its source as the unofficial, keyless Yahoo Chart API. It is a provisional benchmark only; no total-return or vendor-quality audit was performed.

## Recommended sequence / decision gates

1. **No-cost metadata/API test first:** use an approved KRX Open API key to request a fixed small sample of already-missing KR securities (including a known delisted code), plus daily index history. This must test whether old delisted securities remain queryable—not just whether active tickers work. Do not run a bulk download until the entitlement, result shape, and coverage are confirmed.
2. **Ask KRX before buying:** request a quote/sample for the historical daily stock and benchmark/action fields above and ask in writing whether delisted codes and terminal consideration are included. If Open API cannot close the 322 delisted names, this is the direct KR path; public HTML rosters alone cannot fill price returns.
3. **For US, prefer Norgate Platinum/Diamond trial only as a coverage probe:** verify exact asset identity mapping for the 494 absent tickers, daily S&P 500 membership, split/dividend adjustments, delisting/merger consideration, and local Python/Mac export workflow. Trial’s two-year depth is insufficient for the full historical test; do not infer full repair from a trial sample. If the license lifetime/deletion requirements conflict with the project’s retention model, use CRSP through an entitled institution or negotiate a written license instead.
4. **Before any strategy test:** reconcile the 102 repeated KR codes as issuer/security spells, verify KR date-basis and corporate actions, replace or independently validate the US Wikipedia-derived constituent history, and define permanent-ID mappings and terminal-event treatment. Freeze fresh benchmark/data snapshots and hashes. Missing coverage should be reported, not imputed as a zero or silently dropped.

## What remains unresolved

- KRX Open API’s public service list establishes 2010 start dates but does not establish retrieval of all subsequently delisted instruments, total-return index availability, or how adjusted prices are represented. KRX must answer those product-specific questions.
- No lawful, free source was verified that simultaneously supplies complete KR and US historical daily prices, delisted/security-identity histories, corporate actions, terminal proceeds, and authoritative PIT S&P 500 membership. Scraping Yahoo or substituting today’s constituents does not close those gaps.
- The local SPY history is from an unofficial endpoint; there is no corresponding KRX benchmark file in the mirror. Neither currently constitutes a validated matched total-return benchmark pair.
- Purchase choice, permitted retention period, access entitlement, and data-vendor redistribution rights remain unapproved and unverified. No purchase, API request, or data transfer was made.
