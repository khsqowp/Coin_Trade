## Part B and random-entry null modules (2026-11-20)

- Added `nullbench.py` with seeded `RandomState(seed + series_index + repetition*1000003)`, six-process fork `Pool`, percentile/p50/p90 helpers, and the mechanical verdict gates (test return strictly above null p90, non-FRAGILE, at least 30 pooled test trades). Each series contributes its simulated test return to each repetition's pooled sum. Direction quotas use the requested strategy long/short mix with nearest-integer allocation. Random candidates are uniform over the split; accepted candidates are processed chronologically and the next candidate is rejected until the preceding candidate's simulated average hold has elapsed. This is a practical sequential approximation because the existing simulator returns aggregate average hold, not per-trade exit indices; exact trade-count matching can fail when the split has too few usable bars.
- Added `partb.py` for T1-T4, six binary filters, causal higher-timeframe EMA alignment, stop-required management grid, configuration enumeration, train-only pooled ranking, test evaluation, and plateau result helper. HTF candle timestamps are interpreted as open times; candle close time equals open+4h or open+1d. Each lower-timeframe signal timestamp is its bar close (open time + 15m/1h/4h); `merge_asof(direction='backward')` aligns the most recent HTF `completed_at <= signal bar close`. Equality is allowed because both bars are closed at that instant. A still-forming HTF candle is excluded.
- Filter subset count is 64. Enabled lag filters share one window axis `{3,8}`; empty subset uses window 8 once. F_regime is a trailing 500-observation percentile, requiring 100 observations during warm-up, then admits the inclusive 20th-80th percentile band.

## Part B full search revision (2026-11-20)

- Replaced the sampled sequential stub with a deterministic exhaustive signal pass. The five filters are `F_bb`, `F_stoch`, `F_stc`, `F_vol`, and `F_regime`; the obsolete HTF filter is not in this requested grid. There are 32 filter subsets. Nine trigger-parameter cases span T1 `{N=3,5}`, T2 `{level=20,25,30}`, T3 `{level=10,25,40}`, and T4 `{rsi=14}`. The shared window axis `{3,8}` applies to nonempty subsets, and the empty subset is evaluated once at window 8: 9 x (1 + 31 x 2) = 567 Phase 1 signal configurations.
- Phase 1 uses the fixed 5% stop, 4% target, 48-bar hold, zero adds management across the 12 symbols at 1h and 4h (24 series). It requires at least 30 pooled training trades and ranks by the same mean-per-series robust score (`total_return - abs(max_dd) + 0.25 * min(PF,5)`). Stable ties use trade count then canonical configuration identity. Up to 25 distinct eligible signal configurations enter Phase 2. Management axes are six stop choices, three targets, three hold durations, two add counts, and three add spacings, giving 324 configurations. Phase 2 evaluates all 25 x 324 settings on train and selects combined, long-only and short-only winners independently; untouched test slices are evaluated only after selection.
- Indicator banks are built once per series and unique trigger parameter/window pair in the parent process and inherited read-only by six fork workers. Worker errors include signal configuration, management dictionary, side, and series. Timestamped progress logs include completed task count and ETA. The `B` stage is guarded by `--max-minutes`; its `.B.done` marker is written only on successful completion, so completed stages are skipped on normal resume. Quick mode is explicitly a synthetic wiring smoke test and relaxes the 30-trade gate because its short synthetic series may have no eligible strategy.
- Plateau evaluates axial neighbors of the selected combined winner across stop, target, hold, add count, add spacing, each of five filter toggles, trigger parameter, and window when that axis exists. At least 70% of neighbors must score at least 50% of winner robust score for `ROBUST`; otherwise it is `FRAGILE`. Outputs are in `results_v2/partb_*.csv`; `partb_result.json` carries winner variants and pooled train/test metrics, `partb_latest_winner.csv` carries per-symbol/timeframe test results, and `partb_null_handoff.json` carries split bounds, exact test entry counts, direction mix, and management.
- WF uses rolling 12-month train / 3-month test windows every three months. Every window re-ranks the complete 567-config signal grid using the cheap Phase 1 management on that window's training slices, keeps up to five eligible configs, then searches 12 deterministically spaced management finalists. Global Phase 1 winners are not used in WF selection. Outputs are `partb_wf.csv` and `partb_wf_summary.csv`.
- Split audit caught and fixed an earlier runner wiring error: global Phase 1/2 worker bounds initially pointed at TEST. Those first two full timings (238.164 s and 243.933 s) and their generated outputs were discarded as invalid. The accepted run uses `(0, 60% cut)` for every global training selection and evaluates untouched `(60% cut, end)` afterward. WF uses each rolling training window for selection and its following three-month slice for evaluation.
- Real-data timing: BTCUSDT 1h, 42,791 bars, 16 unique indicator banks took 4.055 s; 50 simulations on its 25,674-bar train slice took 0.660 s. The corrected full real Part B stage used six workers, 567 Phase 1 tasks, 8,100 Phase 2 config/management tasks, 16 plateau neighbors and 15 WF windows, and completed in 284.541 s. The WF task logs include 15 separate in-window phase-1 ranks and finalist searches. This accepted end-to-end measurement supersedes earlier projections.
